Ready to discover how sacrifices lead to rewards? Let's dive in!

## Overview
You can't catch a wolf without sacrificing a child.

## Phrase
**舍不得孩子套不住狼** — shě bù de hái zi tào bù zhù láng
## Characters


舍 (shě) — to give up/part with  
不 (bù) — not  
得 (de) — indicates possibility  
孩子 (hái zi) — child  
套 (tào) — to catch/ensnare  
不住 (bù zhù) — cannot hold/keep  
狼 (láng) — wolf


## Origin
Once, a farmer wanted to protect his sheep from a hungry wolf. However, he was afraid to let go of his child, who was playing close by. After much hesitation, he realized that to save his sheep, he needed to lure the wolf away, even if it meant risking his child’s safety for a moment. This story emphasizes that sometimes sacrifices are necessary for greater rewards.

## Examples
- 如果你想要成功，就要舍不得孩子套不住狼。<br>If you want to succeed, you have to be willing to make sacrifices.
- 她一直犹豫投资，但舍不得孩子套不住狼，最终决定下手。<br>She hesitated to invest, but realizing you can't catch a wolf without sacrificing a child, she finally decided to go for it.
- 在事业上，有时候舍不得孩子套不住狼是必要的。<br>In business, sometimes making sacrifices is necessary to achieve your goals.

## Closing
舍不得孩子套不住狼 means you can't catch a wolf without sacrificing a child.

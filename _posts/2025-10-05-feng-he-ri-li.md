> Today, we’re exploring a phrase that paints a picture of lovely weather.

## Overview
A gentle breeze and a beautiful sun; perfect weather.

## Phrase
**风和日丽** — fēng hé rì lì
## Characters


风 (fēng) — wind  
和 (hé) — harmony or peaceful  
日 (rì) — sun  
丽 (lì) — beautiful


## Origin
This idiom originates from ancient Chinese literature, where poets often described idyllic scenes of nature. It evokes a calm and pleasant atmosphere, often used in poetry to set a serene mood. Over time, it has become a common expression in everyday conversation to describe delightful weather.

## Examples
- 今天天气真好，风和日丽！<br>The weather is great today, with a gentle breeze and beautiful sun!
- 在这样的风和日丽的日子里，我们去野餐吧！<br>On a day like this, with perfect weather, let’s go for a picnic!
- 风和日丽的春天总是让人心情愉快。<br>A beautiful sunny day in spring always lifts people's spirits.

> Today, we explore a phrase that captures the essence of non-stop talking.

## Overview
Endlessly chattering; talking non-stop.

## Phrase
**喋喋不休** — dié dié bù xiū
## Characters


喋 (dié) — to chatter  
喋 (dié) — to chatter  
不 (bù) — not  
休 (xiū) — to stop


## Origin
The idiom originated from ancient Chinese literature where it described people who had nothing significant to say but continued to talk. Their incessant chatter was often seen as annoying or unnecessary. Over time, it became a common expression to describe those who just wouldn't stop talking.

## Examples
- 他总是喋喋不休，没完没了。<br>He is always chattering endlessly, never stopping.
- 在会议上，有些人喋喋不休，浪费了时间。<br>In the meeting, some people talked non-stop, wasting time.
- 她的喋喋不休让我感到很疲惫。<br>Her endless chatter makes me feel very tired.

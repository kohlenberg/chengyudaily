## Overview
Blue comes from indigo, meaning students surpass their teachers.

## Phrase
**青出于蓝** — qīng chū yú lán
## Characters


青 (qīng) — blue or green  
出 (chū) — to come out  
于 (yú) — from  
蓝 (lán) — indigo


## Origin
This idiom originates from ancient dyeing techniques where blue dye is made from the indigo plant. While the dye is blue, the plant itself is indigo. This highlights how the product can be more vibrant than its source, symbolizing how students can excel beyond their mentors. It beautifully captures the essence of growth and learning.

## Examples
- 他在音乐上青出于蓝。<br>He has surpassed his teacher in music.
- 这位年轻画家的作品青出于蓝。<br>The works of this young artist surpass those of his teacher.
- 她的表现青出于蓝，令人惊讶。<br>Her performance surpassed expectations, leaving everyone amazed.

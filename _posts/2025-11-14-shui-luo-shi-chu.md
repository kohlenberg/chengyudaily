> Today, we're diving into a phrase about uncovering hidden truths.

## Overview
When the water recedes, the rocks appear; revealing the truth.

## Phrase
**水落石出** — shuǐ luò shí chū
## Characters


水 (shuǐ) — water  
落 (luò) — to fall/recede  
石 (shí) — stone/rock  
出 (chū) — to emerge/come out


## Origin
This idiom originates from the natural observation of rivers and streams. When the water level lowers, rocks and stones that were once hidden beneath the surface become visible. This phenomenon serves as a metaphor for revealing the truth in situations where it may have been obscured. Over time, it has come to symbolize the idea that the truth will always come to light, no matter how well it is hidden.

## Examples
- 在调查中，真相终于水落石出。<br>The truth finally emerged during the investigation.
- 当他公开了证据，大家都说水落石出了。<br>When he revealed the evidence, everyone said the truth was out.
- 时间会证明一切，水落石出。<br>Time will prove everything; the truth will be revealed.

Ever wondered how a phrase about wealth can also reflect prosperity in life?

## Overview
A hall filled with gold and jade; representing wealth and abundance.

## Phrase
**金玉满堂** — jīn yù mǎn táng
## Characters


金 (jīn) — gold  
玉 (yù) — jade  
满 (mǎn) — full  
堂 (táng) — hall


## Origin
In ancient times, gold and jade were symbols of wealth and status. The phrase originated from a historical context where the rich would display these precious items in their homes to showcase their affluence. Over time, it evolved into a metaphor for abundance in both material wealth and life’s blessings.

## Examples
- 他的家里金玉满堂，生活非常富裕。<br>His home is filled with gold and jade; they live very richly.
- 她的成功让我们都感到金玉满堂。<br>Her success makes us all feel prosperous.
- 这座城市金玉满堂，吸引了很多投资者。<br>This city is rich and prosperous, attracting many investors.

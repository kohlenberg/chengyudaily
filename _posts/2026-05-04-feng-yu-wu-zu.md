## Overview
No obstacles from wind and rain; perseverance through difficulties.

## Phrase
**风雨无阻** — fēng yǔ wú zǔ
## Characters


风 (fēng) — wind  
雨 (yǔ) — rain  
无 (wú) — no  
阻 (zǔ) — obstruction


## Origin
This idiom originates from the idea that regardless of weather conditions, a person should continue on their path without giving in to challenges. It emphasizes determination and resilience, often used to encourage others to keep moving forward despite obstacles.

## Examples
- 只要有梦想，风雨无阻，奋斗就是成功。<br>As long as there are dreams, no obstacles from wind and rain, striving is success.
- 他每天都去跑步，风雨无阻。<br>He runs every day, no matter the wind and rain.
- 我们的团队风雨无阻地完成了项目。<br>Our team completed the project without any obstacles from wind and rain.

## Overview
Speaking without thinking; making unwarranted claims.

## Phrase
**信口开河** — xìn kǒu kāi hé
## Characters


信 (xìn) — to believe; to trust  
口 (kǒu) — mouth  
开 (kāi) — to open  
河 (hé) — river


## Origin
This idiom originates from the idea that when someone talks freely, their words flow out like a river—unstoppable and unfiltered. It suggests that such unrestrained speech can lead to spreading misinformation or making irresponsible claims. Originally, it was used to warn against reckless talk that could mislead others.

## Examples
- 他总是信口开河，根本没有证据。<br>He always speaks carelessly, without any evidence at all.
- 在会议上，她信口开河，结果让大家都很困惑。<br>During the meeting, she spoke without thinking, leaving everyone confused.
- 不要信口开河，最好先查清事实。<br>Don't speak carelessly; it's best to check the facts first.

Today, we explore the idea of bravery and reward in Chinese culture.

## Overview
You must take risks to achieve great rewards.

## Phrase
**不入虎穴，焉得虎子** — bù rù hǔ xué, yān dé hǔ zǐ
## Characters


不 (bù) — not  
入 (rù) — enter  
虎 (hǔ) — tiger  
穴 (xué) — den  
， (,) — [pause]  
焉 (yān) — how  
得 (dé) — can/get  
虎 (hǔ) — tiger  
子 (zǐ) — cub


## Origin
This idiom originates from a tale about a brave hunter who wanted to catch tiger cubs. He realized that to succeed, he needed to enter the tiger's den, which was fraught with danger. The story teaches us that without facing challenges, we cannot hope to gain valuable rewards. It's a reminder of the bravery required to achieve great things.

## Examples
- 不入虎穴，焉得虎子，只有勇敢的人才能成功。<br>You must take risks to achieve success; only brave people can succeed.
- 如果你想创业，必须冒险，不入虎穴，焉得虎子。<br>If you want to start a business, you must take risks; you can't achieve rewards without taking chances.
- 她总是说，不入虎穴，焉得虎子，这激励了我去追求梦想。<br>She always says, 'You must take risks to achieve great rewards,' which inspires me to pursue my dreams.

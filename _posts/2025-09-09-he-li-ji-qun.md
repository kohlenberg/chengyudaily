## Overview
A crane standing among a flock of chickens; referring to someone outstanding in a group.

## Phrase
**鹤立鸡群** — hè lì jī qún
## Characters


鹤 (hè) — crane  
立 (lì) — to stand  
鸡 (jī) — chicken  
群 (qún) — group


## Origin
In ancient China, cranes were seen as elegant and noble creatures, often associated with longevity and wisdom. When a crane stands among chickens, it stands out due to its height and grace. This image inspired the idiom to describe someone who shines brightly in a group, making a clear distinction from the others around them.

## Examples
- 他在班上就像鹤立鸡群。<br>He is like a crane standing among chickens in the class.
- 她的才华让她在同事中鹤立鸡群。<br>Her talent makes her stand out among her colleagues like a crane among chickens.
- 在这一场比赛中，他的表现鹤立鸡群。<br>In this competition, his performance was outstanding like a crane in a flock of chickens.

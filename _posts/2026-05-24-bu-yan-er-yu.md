> Today, we explore an idiom that highlights the clarity of understanding without words.

## Overview
It is obvious without needing to say.

## Phrase
**不言而喻** — bù yán ér yù
## Characters


不 (bù) — not  
言 (yán) — to speak  
而 (ér) — and  
喻 (yù) — to understand


## Origin
This idiom originates from ancient times when scholars would often discuss philosophical ideas. They believed that some truths were self-evident and did not require elaboration. The phrase captures the essence of those moments when understanding is instant and clear without the need for words.

## Examples
- 这道题的答案不言而喻。<br>The answer to this question is obvious.
- 他们的友谊不言而喻。<br>Their friendship is clear without needing to say a word.
- 这件事情的结果不言而喻。<br>The outcome of this matter is evident.

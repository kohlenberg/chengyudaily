> Today, we explore the wisdom behind silence and action.

## Overview
Not making a sound until necessary; staying silent until action is required.

## Phrase
**不鸣则已** — bù míng zé yǐ
## Characters


不 (bù) — not  
鸣 (míng) — to make a sound  
则 (zé) — then  
已 (yǐ) — already/stop


## Origin
In ancient times, there was a famous archer who never boasted about his skills. When he shot arrows, he was so precise that he would only speak when he was sure of his aim. His silence spoke volumes, and when he did take action, everyone took notice. This idiom reminds us that sometimes, it's better to let our actions do the talking.

## Examples
- 他平时不鸣则已，一开口就让大家惊讶。<br>He usually stays silent, but when he speaks, everyone is amazed.
- 她不鸣则已，但一旦展现才华，大家都佩服她。<br>She stays quiet, but once she shows her talent, everyone admires her.
- 我觉得不鸣则已，行动才是最重要的。<br>I believe that silence is golden; actions are what truly matter.

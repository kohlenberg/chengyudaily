## Overview
unyielding and unbending; resolute in the face of adversity

## Phrase
**不屈不挠** — bù qū bù náo
## Characters


不 (bù) — not  
屈 (qū) — to bend  
不 (bù) — not  
挠 (náo) — to scratch/yield


## Origin
The idiom comes from ancient Chinese literature, where it was used to describe heroes who faced overwhelming challenges but never gave up. It highlights the spirit of perseverance, particularly in battles or difficult situations. This spirit of resilience is celebrated in various Chinese cultures and is often encouraged in personal and professional endeavors.

## Examples
- 他在困难面前不屈不挠。<br>He remains unyielding in the face of difficulties.
- 她不屈不挠地追求自己的梦想。<br>She resolutely pursues her dreams without yielding.
- 这个团队在挑战中展现了不屈不挠的精神。<br>This team demonstrated an unyielding spirit in the face of challenges.

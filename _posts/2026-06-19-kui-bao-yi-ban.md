> Today, we uncover how a glimpse can lead to misunderstanding.

## Overview
Seeing only a part of something; limited understanding.

## Phrase
**窥豹一斑** — kuī bào yī bān
## Characters


窥 (kuī) — to peek  
豹 (bào) — leopard  
一 (yī) — one  
斑 (bān) — spot or mark


## Origin
In ancient China, there was a story about a person who saw only a part of a leopard—just one of its spots. This person thought they understood the whole animal based on that limited view. The saying evolved to remind people that seeing only a small part can lead to misconceptions about the whole picture.

## Examples
- 他只看了报告的一部分，就认为这个项目失败了。<br>He only looked at part of the report and thought the project had failed.
- 不要只听一面之词，应该全面了解情况。<br>Don't just listen to one side; you should understand the situation comprehensively.
- 我们不能仅凭这一点信息就做出决定。<br>We can't make a decision based on just this piece of information.

## Closing
窥豹一斑 means seeing only a part of something and having a limited understanding.

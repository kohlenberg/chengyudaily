> Today, we dive into a phrase that vividly describes suffering and hardship.

## Overview
Deep water and intense heat; a situation of extreme hardship.

## Phrase
**水深火热** — shuǐ shēn huǒ rè
## Characters


水 (shuǐ) — water  
深 (shēn) — deep  
火 (huǒ) — fire  
热 (rè) — hot or heat


## Origin
This idiom originates from ancient literature that describes the perilous conditions faced by those in war or disaster. It paints a picture of someone caught between the dangers of deep water and raging fire, emphasizing the dire straits one can find themselves in. Over time, it has come to symbolize any extreme hardship people endure in life.

## Examples
- 他在公司裁员后，感到水深火热。<br>After the layoffs at the company, he felt like he was in deep water and intense heat.
- 这个城市的贫困问题让很多居民生活在水深火热中。<br>The poverty problem in this city has left many residents living in deep water and intense heat.
- 在这场疫情中，许多人经历了水深火热的生活。<br>During this pandemic, many people have experienced life in deep water and intense heat.

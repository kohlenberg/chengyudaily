## Overview
Three visits to a thatched cottage; shows great respect and determination to recruit talent.

## Phrase
**三顾茅庐** — sān gù máo lú
## Characters


三 (sān) — three  
顾 (gù) — visit  
茅 (máo) — thatched  
庐 (lú) — cottage


## Origin
During the Three Kingdoms period, the warlord Liu Bei was eager to recruit the brilliant strategist Zhuge Liang. Liu Bei made three trips to Zhuge Liang's humble abode, demonstrating his determination and respect. This persistence eventually paid off, as Zhuge Liang became one of Liu Bei's most trusted advisors.

## Examples
- 他为了找到合适的人才，三顾茅庐。<br>He made three visits to recruit the right talent.
- 老板为了请到这个专家，真是三顾茅庐。<br>The boss really went out of his way to recruit this expert.
- 在求学的路上，我们都需要三顾茅庐的精神。<br>On the path of learning, we all need the spirit of persistence.

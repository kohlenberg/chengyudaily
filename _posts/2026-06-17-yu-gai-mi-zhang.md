Ever tried to hide something but ended up drawing more attention to it?

## Overview
The more you try to cover it up, the more it stands out.

## Phrase
**欲盖弥彰** — yù gài mí zhāng
## Characters


欲 (yù) — to desire  
盖 (gài) — to cover  
弥 (mí) — to fill  
彰 (zhāng) — to be evident or clear


## Origin
This idiom comes from a historical context where someone attempted to hide their wrongdoing. Instead of remaining unnoticed, their efforts to conceal it only highlighted their guilt. This reflects the irony of trying to suppress something, which often leads to greater visibility instead. Hence, this idiom teaches us that attempts at deception can backfire, drawing even more attention.

## Examples
- 他想掩盖自己的错误，结果欲盖弥彰。<br>He wanted to cover up his mistake, but the more he tried, the more it stood out.
- 她的谎言欲盖弥彰，大家都知道了。<br>Her lies were so obvious that everyone found out.
- 想要隐瞒事实，欲盖弥彰，只会让事情更糟。<br>Trying to hide the truth will only make things worse.

## Closing
欲盖弥彰 means that the more you try to cover something up, the more it becomes evident.

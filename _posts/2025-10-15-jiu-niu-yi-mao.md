## Overview
A tiny part of a much larger whole.

## Phrase
**九牛一毛** — jiǔ niú yī máo
## Characters


九 (jiǔ) — nine  
牛 (niú) — cows  
一 (yī) — one  
毛 (máo) — hair or fur


## Origin
This idiom originates from a time when people noted how insignificant a single hair was in comparison to a herd of nine cows. It became a metaphor for something that is negligible compared to a larger context. The expression emphasizes how small individual contributions can seem against a vast backdrop.

## Examples
- 这份工作对公司的发展来说，九牛一毛。<br>This job is just a tiny part of the company's development.
- 你捐的这点钱，对整个慈善项目来说，九牛一毛。<br>The amount you donated is just a drop in the bucket for the entire charity project.
- 他在团队里的努力，九牛一毛。<br>His efforts in the team are just a small contribution.

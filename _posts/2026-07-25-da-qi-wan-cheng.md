## Overview
Great talents take time to develop.

## Phrase
**大器晚成** — dà qì wǎn chéng
## Characters


大 (dà) — big or great  
器 (qì) — tool or instrument  
晚 (wǎn) — late  
成 (chéng) — to become or to succeed


## Origin
This idiom originates from an ancient Chinese belief that just as a large tool takes time to forge, so too do great talents require time to mature. It reminds us that significant achievements often come after years of hard work and perseverance. Just like a fine wine, some qualities and skills get better with age.

## Examples
- 他虽然年轻，但我相信他会大器晚成。<br>He is still young, but I believe he will achieve greatness in time.
- 有些成功者都是大器晚成，经历了许多磨难。<br>Many successful people are late bloomers, having gone through numerous hardships.
- 不要着急，记住大器晚成的道理。<br>Don't rush; remember the principle that great talents take time to develop.

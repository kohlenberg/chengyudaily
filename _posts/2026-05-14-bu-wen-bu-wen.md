## Overview
To not hear or ask; to be indifferent.

## Phrase
**不闻不问** — bù wén bù wèn
## Characters


不 (bù) — not  
闻 (wén) — to hear  
不 (bù) — not  
问 (wèn) — to ask


## Origin
This idiom dates back to ancient times when people would often turn a blind eye to social issues, preferring to stay uninvolved. It highlights a common tendency to avoid responsibility or engagement with pressing matters. Over time, it became a way to describe someone who is apathetic or indifferent.

## Examples
- 他对同事的困难不闻不问。<br>He is indifferent to his colleague's difficulties.
- 社会问题不应不闻不问。<br>Social issues should not be ignored.
- 这种态度让人觉得他不关心任何事情。<br>This attitude makes people feel he doesn't care about anything.

## Overview
Not afraid of ten thousand, just afraid of one in ten thousand.

## Phrase
**不怕一万，就怕万一** — bù pà yī wàn, jiù pà wàn yī
## Characters


不 (bù) — not  
怕 (pà) — afraid  
一万 (yī wàn) — ten thousand  
， (,) — comma  
就 (jiù) — just  
怕 (pà) — afraid  
万一 (wàn yī) — one in ten thousand


## Origin
This idiom originated from a time when people faced unpredictable challenges. It emphasizes the importance of being prepared for unexpected situations, even when they seem rare. Over time, it has become a reminder that caution in the face of uncertainty is wise.

## Examples
- 在做任何投资之前，不怕一万，就怕万一。<br>Before making any investment, don’t be afraid of the large numbers, just be cautious about the unlikely risks.
- 虽然计划很完美，但不怕一万，就怕万一。<br>Although the plan is perfect, we should be cautious about the unlikely problems.
- 上课时，不怕一万，就怕万一，要准备好所有材料。<br>During class, don’t fear the many topics, just be ready for the unexpected questions.

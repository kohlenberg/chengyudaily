> Today, we explore a phrase that reminds us to appreciate our roots.

## Overview
Remember the source of the water you drink.

## Phrase
**饮水思源** — yǐn shuǐ sī yuán
## Characters


饮 (yǐn) — to drink  
水 (shuǐ) — water  
思 (sī) — to think  
源 (yuán) — source


## Origin
This idiom originates from a poem by the ancient philosopher Confucius. He emphasized gratitude and the importance of acknowledging those who have contributed to our lives. Over time, it became a common saying to remind people to be thankful for their origins and support.

## Examples
- 我们在成功时，不要忘记饮水思源。<br>We should not forget to remember our roots when we achieve success.
- 这个节日是为了饮水思源，感谢我们的祖先。<br>This festival is to remember the source and thank our ancestors.
- 她总是饮水思源，感恩父母的养育。<br>She always remembers her roots and is grateful for her parents' upbringing.

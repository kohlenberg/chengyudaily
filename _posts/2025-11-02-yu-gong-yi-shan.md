Welcome to Chengyu Bites — your quick summary on Chinese 成语. Ever heard how determination can move mountains? Let's dive into an inspiring story. The phrase is: 愚公移山, yú gōng yí shān.

## Overview
Literally 'The Foolish Old Man Moves Mountains'; figuratively, it means perseverance can overcome obstacles.

## Phrase
**愚公移山** — yú gōng yí shān
## Characters


愚 (yú) — foolish  
公 (gōng) — old man  
移 (yí) — to move  
山 (shān) — mountain


## Origin
Once upon a time, there was an old man named Yu Gong who lived near two large mountains that blocked his way. He decided to move the mountains, one shovel at a time, despite being mocked by others. His relentless spirit inspired many, and eventually, even the gods took notice and helped him. This story illustrates the power of determination and hard work.

## Examples
- 他相信愚公移山的精神，永远不会放弃。<br>He believes in the spirit of Yu Gong and will never give up.
- 在面对困难时，我们应该学习愚公移山，勇往直前。<br>When facing difficulties, we should learn from Yu Gong and move forward bravely.
- 愚公移山告诉我们，只要努力，就能克服任何障碍。<br>The story of Yu Gong teaches us that with effort, we can overcome any obstacle.

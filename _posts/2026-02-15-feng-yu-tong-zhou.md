## Overview
Literally 'wind and rain, same boat'; figuratively 'to share hardships together'.

## Phrase
**风雨同舟** — fēng yǔ tóng zhōu
## Characters


风 (fēng) — wind  
雨 (yǔ) — rain  
同 (tóng) — together  
舟 (zhōu) — boat


## Origin
This idiom originates from a time when people had to band together to face natural disasters. It emphasizes the importance of unity and support during tough times. Just like being in a boat during a storm, we need to rely on one another to get through challenges together.

## Examples
- 在困难时期，我们应该风雨同舟。<br>During tough times, we should stand together.
- 在这个项目中，大家风雨同舟，才能取得成功。<br>In this project, everyone must work together to achieve success.
- 朋友们，风雨同舟，共同面对挑战！<br>Friends, let's share the hardships and face the challenges together!

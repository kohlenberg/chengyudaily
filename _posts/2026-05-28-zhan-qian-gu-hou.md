## Overview
To look ahead and look back; to consider all aspects before making a decision.

## Phrase
**瞻前顾后** — zhān qián gù hòu
## Characters


瞻 (zhān) — to look at; to gaze at  
前 (qián) — front; ahead  
顾 (gù) — to look back; to consider  
后 (hòu) — back; behind


## Origin
In ancient times, a wise strategist advised a king to always assess both the past and future when making plans. He emphasized that understanding previous mistakes could prevent future failures. Thus, the phrase evolved to remind people to look both ways before proceeding in life.

## Examples
- 在做决定之前，我总是瞻前顾后。<br>Before making a decision, I always look ahead and look back.
- 她在选择职业时瞻前顾后，考虑了很多因素。<br>She looked ahead and looked back when choosing her career, considering many factors.
- 在投资之前，我们应该瞻前顾后，仔细分析市场。<br>Before investing, we should look ahead and look back, carefully analyzing the market.

## Overview
A disorganized crowd; a rabble.

## Phrase
**乌合之众** — wū hé zhī zhòng
## Characters


乌 (wū) — crow  
合 (hé) — to gather  
之 (zhī) — possessive particle  
众 (zhòng) — crowd or multitude


## Origin
The idiom originates from a historical context where a disorganized group of people, much like crows that gather without purpose, was used to describe an unruly mob. This imagery emphasizes the lack of cohesion and effectiveness within such a group. Over time, it has come to represent any assembly of people that lacks discipline or organization.

## Examples
- 这场示威活动完全是乌合之众。<br>This protest is completely a disorganized crowd.
- 在没有领导的情况下，他们成了乌合之众。<br>Without leadership, they became a rabble.
- 面对危机，乌合之众不能解决问题。<br>In the face of a crisis, a disorganized crowd can’t solve the problem.

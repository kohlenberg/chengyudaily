> Today, we explore a phrase that captures the essence of global connections.

## Overview
All corners of the world; everywhere.

## Phrase
**五湖四海** — wǔ hú sì hǎi
## Characters


五 (wǔ) — five  
湖 (hú) — lakes  
四 (sì) — four  
海 (hǎi) — seas


## Origin
This idiom originates from the classic text 'Shui Hu Zhuan', which describes a gathering of people from various regions. The idea is that no matter where you come from, everyone can connect and become friends, just like how lakes and seas are interconnected. This reflects a spirit of unity and inclusivity.

## Examples
- 他在五湖四海都有朋友。<br>He has friends all over the world.
- 这位旅行者走遍了五湖四海。<br>This traveler has been everywhere.
- 我们的文化影响五湖四海。<br>Our culture influences all corners of the globe.

## Closing
Remember, 五湖四海 means 'all corners of the world'; it's about connection and friendship.

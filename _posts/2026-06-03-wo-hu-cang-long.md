## Overview
Hidden talents or powerful forces lying in wait.

## Phrase
**卧虎藏龙** — wò hǔ cáng lóng
## Characters


卧 (wò) — to lie down  
虎 (hǔ) — tiger  
藏 (cáng) — to hide  
龙 (lóng) — dragon


## Origin
This idiom originates from a famous novel during the Ming Dynasty called 'The Knight-Errant.' It described a time when there were hidden heroes and talents not yet recognized. Just like a tiger that lies low and a dragon that hides, these figures could spring into action when the time was right.

## Examples
- 在这个小镇上，卧虎藏龙，有很多隐秘的高手。<br>In this small town, there are hidden talents, many secret masters.
- 这位年轻的艺术家真是卧虎藏龙，作品令人惊叹。<br>This young artist is truly a hidden talent; their work is astonishing.
- 他看起来普通，但其实卧虎藏龙，能力非凡。<br>He seems ordinary, but in fact, he is a hidden talent with extraordinary abilities.

## Overview
With unified will, we can build a strong fortress.

## Phrase
**众志成城** — zhòng zhì chéng chéng
## Characters


众 (zhòng) — crowd or many  
志 (zhì) — will or aspiration  
成 (chéng) — to become or to build  
城 (chéng) — city or fortress


## Origin
This idiom originates from a historical context during the Warring States period in China, where various states fought for control. It was noted that when people unite with a common goal, they can form a strong defense, much like building a city out of their collective will. This concept became a powerful symbol for cooperation and solidarity among people.

## Examples
- 大家齐心协力，众志成城，必定能战胜困难。<br>Everyone working together, united in will, can surely overcome difficulties.
- 在这个项目中，众志成城的精神激励着每一个人。<br>The spirit of unity inspires everyone in this project.
- 面对挑战，我们要众志成城，共同努力。<br>Facing challenges, we must unite and work hard together.

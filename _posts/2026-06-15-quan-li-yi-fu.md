## Overview
to give one's all; to spare no effort

## Phrase
**全力以赴** — quán lì yǐ fù
## Characters


全 (quán) — all  
力 (lì) — strength  
以 (yǐ) — to use  
赴 (fù) — to go to or to commit


## Origin
This idiom originates from a historical context where armies would commit all their resources and strength to a battle, leaving no room for retreat. It emphasizes a mindset of total dedication and effort, no matter the challenges ahead. Over time, it has been adopted in various contexts, including personal goals and teamwork.

## Examples
- 我们必须全力以赴才能赢得比赛。<br>We must give our all to win the game.
- 在这个项目中，他全力以赴，结果非常成功。<br>He gave his all in this project, and the results were very successful.
- 如果想通过考试，学生们应该全力以赴。<br>If they want to pass the exam, students should give their all.

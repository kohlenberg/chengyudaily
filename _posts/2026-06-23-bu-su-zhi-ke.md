## Overview
an unexpected guest; someone who arrives uninvited

## Phrase
**不速之客** — bù sù zhī kè
## Characters


不 (bù) — not  
速 (sù) — speed/fast  
之 (zhī) — possessive particle  
客 (kè) — guest/visitor


## Origin
In ancient China, it was common for hosts to prepare extensively for guests. A visitor who arrived unannounced could disrupt the host's plans and cause chaos. Over time, this phrase began to symbolize not just unexpected guests but any surprise that can throw off one's routine.

## Examples
- 他是一个不速之客。<br>He is an unexpected guest.
- 这种不速之客真让人感到惊讶。<br>This kind of unexpected guest really surprises people.
- 我们准备了很多食物，但他是个不速之客。<br>We prepared a lot of food, but he was an uninvited guest.

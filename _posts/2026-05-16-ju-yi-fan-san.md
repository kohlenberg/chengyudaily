## Overview
Learn one thing and infer three others.

## Phrase
**举一反三** — jǔ yī fǎn sān
## Characters


举 (jǔ) — to raise or to lift  
一 (yī) — one  
反 (fǎn) — to reflect or to reverse  
三 (sān) — three


## Origin
This idiom originates from Chinese educational philosophy, where students are encouraged to draw broader conclusions from individual teachings. It emphasizes critical thinking and applying knowledge to different contexts, promoting a deeper understanding of concepts. The idea is that if you understand one principle well, you can figure out several related ideas.

## Examples
- 学完这道题后，我能举一反三。<br>After learning this problem, I can infer three others.
- 他用这个方法举一反三，解决了其他问题。<br>He used this method to infer three others and solved the other problems.
- 老师鼓励我们举一反三，掌握更多知识。<br>The teacher encourages us to learn one thing and infer three others to master more knowledge.

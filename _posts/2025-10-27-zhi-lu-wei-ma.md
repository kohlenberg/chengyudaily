Ever heard of calling a deer a horse? Let’s explore the meaning behind this intriguing phrase.

## Overview
Pointing at a deer and calling it a horse; to distort facts.

## Phrase
**指鹿为马** — zhǐ lù wéi mǎ
## Characters


指 (zhǐ) — to point  
鹿 (lù) — deer  
为 (wéi) — to be  
马 (mǎ) — horse


## Origin
This idiom originates from a historical event during the Qin Dynasty. A powerful official named Zhao Gao once pointed at a deer and insisted it was a horse to test the loyalty of his courtiers. Those who went along with his claim were seen as loyal, while those who disagreed faced dire consequences. This act symbolized the manipulation of truth for personal gain.

## Examples
- 他说的都是谎言，正是指鹿为马。<br>He is just telling lies, it’s truly pointing at a deer and calling it a horse.
- 在这个公司里，有些人喜欢指鹿为马。<br>In this company, some people enjoy distorting the truth.
- 我们不能容忍指鹿为马的行为。<br>We cannot tolerate the act of distorting the facts.

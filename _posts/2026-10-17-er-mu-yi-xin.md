## Overview
A fresh perspective or new outlook.

## Phrase
**耳目一新** — ěr mù yī xīn
## Characters


耳 (ěr) — ear  
目 (mù) — eye  
一 (yī) — one  
新 (xīn) — new


## Origin
In ancient China, the phrase originates from the idea that when one hears and sees something new, it can completely change their understanding and perception of the world. This concept emphasizes the importance of new experiences in gaining fresh insights. People often used it when they encountered innovative ideas or perspectives that opened their minds to new possibilities.

## Examples
- 这本书让我耳目一新。<br>This book opened my eyes to new ideas.
- 他的演讲让大家耳目一新。<br>His speech gave everyone a fresh perspective.
- 在这次旅行中，我耳目一新。<br>I had a fresh outlook during this trip.

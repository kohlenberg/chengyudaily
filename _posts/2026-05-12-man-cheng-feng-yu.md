> Today, we explore how a storm of rumors can fill an entire city.

## Overview
A city filled with wind and rain; widespread gossip or rumors.

## Phrase
**满城风雨** — mǎn chéng fēng yǔ
## Characters


满 (mǎn) — full, filled  
城 (chéng) — city, town  
风 (fēng) — wind  
雨 (yǔ) — rain


## Origin
This idiom originates from a historical event where a city was engulfed in chaos due to the spread of false information. As rumors circulated like a storm, the citizens were left in confusion and anxiety. The imagery of wind and rain vividly depicts the turmoil and unrest caused by these whispers.

## Examples
- 最近，关于他辞职的传闻满城风雨。<br>Recently, rumors about his resignation are filling the city.
- 这件事让满城风雨，大家都在讨论。<br>This matter has caused a storm of gossip throughout the city; everyone is talking about it.
- 她的秘密被泄露，满城风雨。<br>Her secret was leaked, and now the city is filled with rumors.

## Overview
Chickens and dogs are restless; chaos ensues.

## Phrase
**鸡犬不宁** — jī quǎn bù níng
## Characters


鸡 (jī) — chicken  
犬 (quǎn) — dog  
不 (bù) — not  
宁 (níng) — peaceful or calm


## Origin
In ancient China, a farmer's home was often filled with the sounds of animals. When something disturbed the peace, like a thief or wild animal, not only would the chickens cluck loudly, but the dogs would bark incessantly too. This chaotic noise reflected the farmer's own state of distress, as he struggled to restore order. The phrase evolved to describe any situation where there is chaos and lack of peace.

## Examples
- 家里有小孩，鸡犬不宁。<br>There are kids at home, and it's complete chaos.
- 这场比赛让观众们鸡犬不宁。<br>This match had the audience in a frenzy.
- 他的到来使得聚会鸡犬不宁。<br>His arrival turned the party into a whirlwind of excitement.

## Overview
Blind people touching an elephant; signifies limited perspectives.

## Phrase
**盲人摸象** — máng rén mō xiàng
## Characters


盲 (máng) — blind  
人 (rén) — person  
摸 (mō) — touch  
象 (xiàng) — elephant


## Origin
In ancient times, a group of blind men encountered an elephant for the first time. Each one touched a different part of the elephant's body, like the trunk, the ear, or the leg. Based on their limited experience, they each described the elephant differently, leading to a heated argument. This story illustrates how our limited views can lead us to misunderstand the bigger picture.

## Examples
- 他只看了报告的一部分，就认为项目很糟糕，这真是盲人摸象。<br>He only looked at part of the report and thought the project was terrible; this is truly 盲人摸象.
- 你不能只听一个人的意见就下结论，这样就像盲人摸象。<br>You can't jump to conclusions based on just one person's opinion; that's like 盲人摸象.
- 在讨论这个复杂的问题时，我们不能盲人摸象。<br>When discussing this complex issue, we can't be like 盲人摸象.

Welcome to Chengyu Bites — your quick summary on Chinese 成语. Ever wondered how the past can guide our future decisions? The phrase is: 前车之鉴, qián chē zhī jiàn.

## Overview
a warning from the past; learn from others' mistakes

## Phrase
**前车之鉴** — qián chē zhī jiàn
## Characters


前 (qián) — front  
车 (chē) — vehicle  
之 (zhī) — of  
鉴 (jiàn) — mirror or lesson


## Origin
This idiom comes from an ancient Chinese proverb about a chariot. It refers to a situation where a previous vehicle had an accident, serving as a warning to those who follow. The lesson is clear: observe the failures of others to avoid making the same mistakes yourself.

## Examples
- 我们应该记住前车之鉴。<br>We should remember the lessons from past mistakes.
- 他的失败是我们前车之鉴。<br>His failure is our lesson to learn from.
- 这次计划要考虑前车之鉴。<br>This plan should take past mistakes into account.

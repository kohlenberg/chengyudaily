> Ever wondered how a simple tube of toothpaste can teach us about patience and effort?

## Overview
Squeezing toothpaste; to do something slowly and reluctantly.

## Phrase
**挤牙膏** — jǐ yágāo
## Characters


挤 (jǐ) — to squeeze  
牙 (yá) — tooth  
膏 (gāo) — paste


## Origin
The idiom originates from the common practice of squeezing toothpaste from a tube. People often try to get the last bit out, doing it little by little. This reflects how in life, some tasks can feel like you're trying to extract every last bit of effort, often requiring patience and persistence.

## Examples
- 他做这个项目像挤牙膏一样，进展缓慢。<br>He is making progress on this project like squeezing toothpaste, slowly.
- 她对这个问题的回答就像挤牙膏，迟迟不肯说出。<br>Her response to the question was like squeezing toothpaste, reluctant to come out.
- 我们的预算被挤牙膏一样，越来越紧。<br>Our budget feels like it's being squeezed like toothpaste, getting tighter and tighter.

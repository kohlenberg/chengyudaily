## Overview
to give without expecting anything in return

## Phrase
**不求回报** — bù qiú huí bào
## Characters


不 (bù) — not  
求 (qiú) — to seek  
回 (huí) — to return  
报 (bào) — to repay


## Origin
This idiom originated from the idea that true kindness does not anticipate rewards. Ancient philosophers believed that genuine acts of kindness should come from the heart, not for personal gain. Over time, this concept became a valued principle in Chinese culture, emphasizing selflessness in relationships and actions.

## Examples
- 他帮助别人，不求回报。<br>He helps others without expecting anything in return.
- 她总是为社区服务，不求回报。<br>She always serves the community without seeking any reward.
- 朋友之间的帮助应该是不求回报的。<br>Help among friends should be without expectations of return.

## Overview
One action, two gains.

## Phrase
**一举两得** — yī jǔ liǎng dé
## Characters

一 (yī) — one  
举 (jǔ) — to lift or to act  
两 (liǎng) — two  
得 (dé) — to gain or to obtain


## Origin
The idiom originates from hunting practices in ancient China. A skilled hunter would aim at two birds with one arrow. If successful, he would not only provide food for himself but also ensure that he could share with his family, thus benefiting both himself and his loved ones. This clever strategy made the phrase popular as a metaphor for efficiency in achieving goals.

## Examples
- 通过学习外语，他一举两得，不仅提升了自己的能力，还交到了很多朋友。<br>By learning a foreign language, he achieved two gains: he not only improved his skills but also made many friends.
- 这项投资让公司一举两得，既增加了利润，又提升了品牌形象。<br>This investment allowed the company to gain two benefits: it increased profits and enhanced the brand image.
- 参加这个活动，她一举两得，既锻炼了身体，又放松了心情。<br>By participating in this event, she achieved two gains: she exercised her body and relaxed her mind.

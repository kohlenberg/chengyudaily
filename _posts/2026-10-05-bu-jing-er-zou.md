## Overview
to spread rapidly without legs; to disseminate quickly

## Phrase
**不胫而走** — bù jìng ér zǒu
## Characters


不 (bù) — not  
胫 (jìng) — shank or leg  
而 (ér) — and  
走 (zǒu) — to walk or run


## Origin
This idiom originates from an ancient tale where information spread like a rumor without anyone actually seeing it travel. It highlights how quickly news can travel, much like a person running without needing legs. This vivid imagery helps convey the idea of fast dissemination.

## Examples
- 这个消息不胫而走，大家都知道了。<br>This news spread rapidly, and everyone knows about it.
- 她的成功故事在学校里不胫而走。<br>Her success story spread quickly throughout the school.
- 这个谣言不胫而走，让人感到惊讶。<br>This rumor spread quickly, surprising everyone.

## Closing
不胫而走 means to spread rapidly without legs; thank you for listening!

> Ever met someone whose words don't match their true feelings? This idiom captures that essence.

## Overview
Words and heart are not in agreement; to say one thing while meaning another.

## Phrase
**口是心非** — kǒu shì xīn fēi
## Characters


口 (kǒu) — mouth  
是 (shì) — is  
心 (xīn) — heart  
非 (fēi) — not


## Origin
This idiom originates from ancient Chinese literature, where it was used to describe a person who speaks deceitfully. It highlights the discrepancy between one's spoken words and their true intentions. In a world where honesty is valued, this phrase serves as a reminder to be wary of those who may not express their true thoughts.

## Examples
- 他对我说他很高兴，口是心非。<br>He told me he was very happy, but his words are not sincere.
- 有些人嘴上说支持你，口是心非。<br>Some people say they support you, but their hearts do not agree.
- 她总是口是心非，让我很困惑。<br>She is always saying one thing but meaning another, which confuses me a lot.

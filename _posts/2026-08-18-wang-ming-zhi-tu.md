Today, we explore a phrase that captures the essence of risk and desperation.

## Overview
Desperate individuals willing to risk everything.

## Phrase
**亡命之徒** — wáng mìng zhī tú
## Characters


亡 (wáng) — to perish or to die  
命 (mìng) — life or fate  
之 (zhī) — of (a possessive particle)  
徒 (tú) — person or individual


## Origin
In ancient China, there were tales of individuals who would go to extreme lengths to escape danger or pursue freedom. These people, often seen as outlaws, were considered to have nothing left to lose, hence their willingness to take great risks. Their stories became legends, leading to the term we use today, which reflects the idea of someone who has become desperate and reckless.

## Examples
- 他是一个亡命之徒，常常违法。<br>He is a desperate individual who often breaks the law.
- 在这样的情况下，你必须变成亡命之徒。<br>In such a situation, you must become a desperate person.
- 他们是亡命之徒，为了自由而战。<br>They are desperate individuals fighting for freedom.

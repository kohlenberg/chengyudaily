## Overview
Do not forget your original intention; stay true to your goals.

## Phrase
**不忘初心** — bù wàng chū xīn
## Characters


不 (bù) — not  
忘 (wàng) — forget  
初 (chū) — original  
心 (xīn) — intention or heart


## Origin
This idiom originated from a speech by a Chinese leader, where he emphasized the importance of remembering one’s original aspirations, especially when facing challenges. It serves as a reminder to keep your initial goals in mind, no matter how difficult the journey may become. Many people have adopted this phrase as a way to inspire themselves and others to persevere.

## Examples
- 我们应该不忘初心，继续努力。<br>We should not forget our original intention and continue to work hard.
- 在成功后，他仍然不忘初心。<br>After achieving success, he still stayed true to his original intention.
- 教师的使命是帮助学生不忘初心。<br>The mission of teachers is to help students stay true to their original intentions.

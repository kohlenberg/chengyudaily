## Overview
Perfectly lifelike; vivid and detailed.

## Phrase
**惟妙惟肖** — wéi miào wéi xiào
## Characters


惟 (wéi) — only, just  
妙 (miào) — wonderful, exquisite  
肖 (xiào) — to resemble, to be like


## Origin
This idiom originally comes from the realm of art, specifically painting and sculpture. It is said that a talented artist could create a work so lifelike that it seems to come alive. The phrase emphasizes the skill involved in capturing the essence of a subject with remarkable detail. Over time, it evolved to describe anything that is strikingly vivid or realistic.

## Examples
- 他的画惟妙惟肖，仿佛可以说话。<br>His painting is so lifelike that it seems to speak.
- 这个雕塑惟妙惟肖，令人惊叹。<br>This sculpture is so vivid that it astonishes everyone.
- 她的表演惟妙惟肖，观众都入迷了。<br>Her performance was so captivatingly realistic that the audience was mesmerized.

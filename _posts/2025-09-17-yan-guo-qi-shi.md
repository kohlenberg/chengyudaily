## Overview
Exaggerated words that don't match reality.

## Phrase
**言过其实** — yán guò qí shí
## Characters

言 (yán) — speech, words  
过 (guò) — too much, excessive  
其 (qí) — its, that  
实 (shí) — reality, truth


## Origin
The phrase originates from ancient texts where scholars discussed the importance of honesty in communication. There was a warning against those who would embellish tales, leading listeners astray. This caution was essential in maintaining trust and credibility in society. Over time, it evolved into a common expression used to call out exaggeration.

## Examples
- 他总是言过其实，让人难以相信。<br>He always exaggerates, making it hard for people to believe him.
- 这篇文章言过其实，缺乏真实的数据。<br>This article is exaggerated, lacking real data.
- 我们应该避免言过其实，以免失去信任。<br>We should avoid exaggeration to prevent losing trust.

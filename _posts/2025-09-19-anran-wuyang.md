## Overview
Safe and sound; unharmed.

## Phrase
**安然无恙** — ānrán wúyàng
## Characters


安 (ān) — safe  
然 (rán) — so, thus  
无 (wú) — without  
恙 (yàng) — harm, injury


## Origin
This idiom originates from the idea of someone returning from a perilous journey without any injuries or problems. In ancient times, travelers faced many dangers, and to arrive home safe and sound was a cause for celebration. It became a common phrase to express relief and joy when someone was unharmed.

## Examples
- 经过一场大风暴，他安然无恙。<br>After a fierce storm, he was safe and sound.
- 这次考试后，虽然我很紧张，但结果还是安然无恙。<br>After this exam, although I was very nervous, the result was still safe and sound.
- 她从车祸中幸存下来，真是安然无恙。<br>She survived the car accident; it’s truly a miracle she is unharmed.

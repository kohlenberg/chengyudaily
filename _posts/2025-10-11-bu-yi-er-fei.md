## Overview
to disappear suddenly and without a trace; to vanish into thin air.

## Phrase
**不翼而飞** — bù yì ér fēi
## Characters


不 (bù) — not  
翼 (yì) — wings  
而 (ér) — and  
飞 (fēi) — to fly


## Origin
This idiom originates from a tale about a clever thief who managed to steal a valuable item overnight. When the owner awoke, he found that the item had vanished as if it had flown away without wings. This phrase captures the essence of something that disappears unexpectedly, leaving no evidence behind.

## Examples
- 他的手机不翼而飞了。<br>His phone vanished into thin air.
- 这些文件在一夜之间不翼而飞。<br>These documents disappeared overnight.
- 这只鸟不翼而飞，让大家很惊讶。<br>The bird flew away mysteriously, surprising everyone.

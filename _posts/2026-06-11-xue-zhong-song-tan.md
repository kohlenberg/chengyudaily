## Overview
Delivering coal in the snow; providing timely help.

## Phrase
**雪中送炭** — xuě zhōng sòng tàn
## Characters


雪 (xuě) — snow  
中 (zhōng) — in  
送 (sòng) — to send  
炭 (tàn) — coal


## Origin
In ancient China, during harsh winters, people would often struggle to find warmth. If someone brought coal to a family caught in a snowstorm, it was a lifesaver. This act of kindness inspired the idiom, highlighting the importance of helping others when they are in dire situations.

## Examples
- 他在我最困难的时候给了我很多帮助，真是雪中送炭。<br>He helped me a lot when I was in the toughest times; he truly delivered coal in the snow.
- 朋友在我失业时支持我，实在是雪中送炭。<br>My friend supported me when I lost my job; it was indeed delivering coal in the snow.
- 医生在疫情期间无私奉献，真是雪中送炭。<br>The doctors selflessly dedicated themselves during the pandemic; they were truly delivering coal in the snow.

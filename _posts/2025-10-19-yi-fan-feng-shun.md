> Today, we’re setting sail with a phrase that promises a smooth journey.

## Overview
Smooth sailing; everything goes well.

## Phrase
**一帆风顺** — yī fān fēng shùn
## Characters


一 (yī) — one  
帆 (fān) — sail  
风 (fēng) — wind  
顺 (shùn) — smooth or favorable


## Origin
This idiom originates from the maritime world where a ship with a single sail moves smoothly when the wind is in its favor. It symbolizes a journey that is free from obstacles, encouraging people to strive for success without difficulties. The phrase is often used to wish someone a smooth and prosperous journey in their endeavors.

## Examples
- 祝你们的旅行一帆风顺！<br>Wishing you a smooth sailing journey!
- 在这次项目中，希望大家都能一帆风顺。<br>In this project, I hope everyone can have smooth sailing.
- 他的新事业开始时就一帆风顺。<br>His new business started off with smooth sailing.

## Closing
So, remember, 一帆风顺 means smooth sailing, wishing you all the best in your journeys!

## Overview
No pain, no gain.

## Phrase
**不打不成器** — bù dǎ bù chéng qì
## Characters


不 (bù) — no, not  
打 (dǎ) — hit, strike  
不 (bù) — no, not  
成 (chéng) — become, turn into  
器 (qì) — tool, device


## Origin
This idiom originates from the ancient practice of metalworking. Craftsmen would strike raw metal repeatedly to shape it into useful tools. Without this rigorous process, the metal would remain useless. This principle extends to life, suggesting that challenges and struggles are essential for personal growth and development.

## Examples
- 你必须努力工作，不打不成器。<br>You must work hard; no pain, no gain.
- 学习新技能的时候，不打不成器。<br>When learning a new skill, no pain, no gain.
- 生活中有很多困难，但不打不成器。<br>There are many difficulties in life, but no pain, no gain.

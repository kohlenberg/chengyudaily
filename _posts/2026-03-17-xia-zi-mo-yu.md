## Overview
Blind man feeling for fish; refers to making guesses without proper knowledge.

## Phrase
**瞎子摸鱼** — xiā zi mō yú
## Characters


瞎 (xiā) — blind  
子 (zi) — person  
摸 (mō) — to feel or touch  
鱼 (yú) — fish


## Origin
This idiom originates from a time when people believed that a blind man might try to catch fish by simply feeling around in the water. Without sight, his chances of success were slim, symbolizing the futility of attempting something without the necessary skills or understanding. It reflects a humorous yet sobering reality about blindly trying to achieve a goal.

## Examples
- 他做这个项目就像瞎子摸鱼。<br>He is working on this project like a blind man feeling for fish.
- 没有准备就参与比赛，简直是瞎子摸鱼。<br>Joining the competition without preparation is just like a blind man feeling for fish.
- 在不懂的情况下做决定，就像瞎子摸鱼。<br>Making decisions without understanding is like a blind man feeling for fish.

## Closing
So, that’s 瞎子摸鱼, which means attempting something without knowledge or insight.

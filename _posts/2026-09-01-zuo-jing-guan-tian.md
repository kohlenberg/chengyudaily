## Overview
To have a limited perspective; to see things narrowly.

## Phrase
**坐井观天** — zuò jǐng guān tiān
## Characters


坐 (zuò) — to sit  
井 (jǐng) — well  
观 (guān) — to observe  
天 (tiān) — sky


## Origin
Long ago, a frog lived in a deep well and believed that the sky was only the small circle above him. One day, a turtle from the sea visited and told him about the vastness of the world beyond his well. The frog was astonished but struggled to believe it. This story teaches us to recognize our limitations and seek broader perspectives.

## Examples
- 他总是坐井观天，觉得自己知道的一切。<br>He always sees things narrowly, thinking he knows everything.
- 不要坐井观天，要多出去看看。<br>Don’t have a limited view; go out and see more.
- 他的观点太狭隘，真是坐井观天。<br>His viewpoint is too narrow; he truly sees things from a limited perspective.

Welcome to Chengyu Bites — your quick summary on Chinese 成语. Ever wondered how a rooster can inspire greatness? Let's find out!

## Overview
Rise to practice at the sound of a rooster crowing; symbolizes diligence and seizing opportunities.

## Phrase
**闻鸡起舞** — wén jī qǐ wǔ
## Characters


闻 (wén) — to hear  
鸡 (jī) — chicken or rooster  
起 (qǐ) — to rise or get up  
舞 (wǔ) — to dance


## Origin
This idiom originates from the story of a famous general, Yue Fei, who was so dedicated to his martial arts training that he would wake up at the sound of a rooster to practice. His commitment to self-improvement and seizing the moment became legendary. The phrase captures the essence of diligence and readiness to act when opportunities arise.

## Examples
- 他每天都闻鸡起舞，努力学习。<br>He practices hard every day, rising at the sound of the rooster.
- 她闻鸡起舞，早起锻炼。<br>She rises to work out early at the sound of the rooster.
- 我们应该闻鸡起舞，把握每一个机会。<br>We should seize every opportunity, rising to the occasion like the rooster.

## Closing
闻鸡起舞 means to rise to practice at the sound of a rooster, symbolizing diligence and seizing opportunities. Thank you for listening to Chengyu Bites! See you next time for another idiom.

Welcome to Chengyu Bites — your quick summary on Chinese 成语. Ever wondered how a cornered dog might react? Let's find out!

## Overview
A desperate person will take drastic actions.

## Phrase
**狗急跳墙** — gǒu jí tiàoqiáng
## Characters


狗 (gǒu) — dog  
急 (jí) — urgent/desperate  
跳 (tiào) — to jump  
墙 (qiáng) — wall


## Origin
The idiom originates from an ancient tale where a dog, cornered by a hunter, jumped over a wall to escape. This act of desperation symbolizes how one might behave when faced with dire circumstances. It teaches us that urgency can lead to unexpected actions.

## Examples
- 他被老板骂得很惨，狗急跳墙，决定辞职。<br>He was scolded harshly by his boss, and in desperation, he decided to resign.
- 她的存款快用光了，狗急跳墙，开始借钱。<br>Her savings were running low, and in desperation, she started borrowing money.
- 面对竞争对手，他狗急跳墙，试图压低价格。<br>Faced with competitors, he took drastic actions, trying to lower prices.

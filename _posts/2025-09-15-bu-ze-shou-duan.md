> Today, we explore the lengths one might go to achieve success.

## Overview
By any means necessary; using any method to achieve a goal.

## Phrase
**不择手段** — bù zé shǒu duàn
## Characters


不 (bù) — not  
择 (zé) — choose  
手 (shǒu) — hand  
段 (duàn) — method or means


## Origin
The phrase 不择手段 originates from the idea that in pursuit of a goal, one may resort to any means necessary, regardless of the morality or ethics involved. It reflects a pragmatic approach to achieving success, often at the expense of principles. This expression is commonly used in various contexts, particularly in business and competitive environments. It serves as a cautionary reminder about the potential consequences of such an approach.

## Examples
- 他为了成功不择手段。<br>He uses any means necessary to succeed.
- 在竞争中，有些人会不择手段。<br>Some people will go to any lengths in competition.
- 这种做法是不择手段的表现。<br>This behavior is a manifestation of using any means necessary.

## Closing
不择手段 means to use any means necessary to achieve a goal.

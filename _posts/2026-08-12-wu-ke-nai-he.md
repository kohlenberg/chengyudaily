## Overview
No alternatives; to have no choice.

## Phrase
**无可奈何** — wú kě nài hé
## Characters


无 (wú) — no  
可 (kě) — can  
奈 (nài) — to do  
何 (hé) — what


## Origin
This idiom originates from a poem in ancient China, where a person finds themselves in an unfortunate situation with no options left. The phrase reflects feelings of powerlessness, as one might face circumstances beyond their control. Over time, it evolved into a commonly used expression when faced with unavoidable difficulties.

## Examples
- 我们已经尽力了，结果还是失败了，真是无可奈何。<br>We did our best, but still failed; it's truly a helpless situation.
- 她的病情很严重，我们无可奈何。<br>Her condition is serious, and we have no choice.
- 面对这样的局势，我感到无可奈何。<br>Faced with this situation, I feel powerless.

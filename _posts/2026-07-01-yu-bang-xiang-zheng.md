## Overview
When the sandpiper and clam fight, a third party benefits.

## Phrase
**鹬蚌相争** — yù bàng xiāng zhēng
## Characters


鹬 (yù) — sandpiper  
蚌 (bàng) — clam  
相 (xiāng) — each other  
争 (zhēng) — to contend or fight


## Origin
In ancient times, a sandpiper and a clam were engaged in a fierce struggle. The sandpiper wanted to eat the clam, while the clam was trying to close its shell to protect itself. Their fight drew the attention of a fisherman, who took advantage of their struggle and caught both. This story illustrates how conflicts can lead to unexpected benefits for outsiders.

## Examples
- 两个企业正在鹬蚌相争，第三者却在旁边获利。<br>Two companies are in a fierce competition, while a third party is profiting on the sidelines.
- 在这场辩论中，鹬蚌相争的局面让观众获益。<br>In this debate, the conflict between two sides benefits the audience.
- 他们的争吵只是鹬蚌相争，最后受益的是其他人。<br>Their argument is just a case of the sandpiper and clam fighting, and the ones who benefit in the end are others.

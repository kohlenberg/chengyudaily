> Today, we're exploring a phrase that expresses the hope for all things to go smoothly.

## Overview
Everything goes as you wish.

## Phrase
**万事如意** — wàn shì rú yì
## Characters


万 (wàn) — ten thousand / all  
事 (shì) — things / affairs  
如 (rú) — like / as  
意 (yì) — will / desire


## Origin
This idiom has roots in traditional Chinese culture, where expressing good wishes for others was very common. It often appears during celebrations, such as weddings, to convey hopes for a happy and successful life. The phrase captures the essence of optimism and well-wishing, making it a staple in greetings and blessings.

## Examples
- 祝你万事如意！<br>Wishing you all your desires come true!
- 在新年期间，大家都说万事如意。<br>During the New Year, everyone says, 'May all things go smoothly.'
- 他的生意越来越好，真是万事如意。<br>His business is getting better and better; truly everything goes as he wishes.

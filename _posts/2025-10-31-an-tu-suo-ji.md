Welcome to Chengyu Bites — your quick summary on Chinese 成语. Ever wondered how a picture can mislead you in your search for success? The phrase is: 按图索骥, àn tú suǒ jì.

## Overview
Searching for a horse based on a drawing; following a plan too rigidly.

## Phrase
**按图索骥** — àn tú suǒ jì
## Characters


按 (àn) — to press or to follow  
图 (tú) — picture or drawing  
索 (suǒ) — to search or look for  
骥 (jì) — steed or horse


## Origin
This idiom comes from the tale of a prince who wanted to find a famous horse. A painter drew a picture of this horse, and the prince sent out his men to find it. However, they only looked for horses that matched the drawing, ignoring other qualities of a good horse. This led them to miss out on many excellent horses that didn’t look exactly like the one in the picture.

## Examples
- 他按图索骥，没有看到其他更好的选择。<br>He searched for a horse based on the drawing and missed out on better options.
- 在工作中，不能只按图索骥，要灵活应变。<br>In work, you can’t just follow the plan rigidly; you need to be adaptable.
- 学习时，不要按图索骥，要理解概念。<br>When studying, don’t just follow the guide; understand the concepts.

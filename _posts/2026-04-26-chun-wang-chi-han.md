## Overview
When the lips are gone, the teeth feel cold; meaning mutual dependence.

## Phrase
**唇亡齿寒** — chún wáng chǐ hán
## Characters


唇 (chún) — lips  
亡 (wáng) — gone/lost  
齿 (chǐ) — teeth  
寒 (hán) — cold


## Origin
This idiom originates from a historical context where a conflict between two neighboring states led to one state losing its protective ally (the lips), resulting in vulnerability (the cold teeth) for the other. The loss of one party directly affected the well-being of the other, illustrating their interdependence.

## Examples
- 朋友之间唇亡齿寒，互相帮助很重要。<br>Among friends, when the lips are gone, the teeth feel cold; it’s important to help each other.
- 企业之间也唇亡齿寒，合作才能共赢。<br>Between businesses, when the lips are gone, the teeth feel cold; cooperation leads to mutual success.
- 在国际关系中，唇亡齿寒的道理同样适用。<br>In international relations, the principle of mutual dependence also applies.

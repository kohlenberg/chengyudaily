Welcome to Chengyu Bites — your quick summary on Chinese 成语. Ever wondered how some people build empires from nothing? The phrase is: 白手起家, bái shǒu qǐ jiā.

## Overview
Starting from scratch; building a business without any initial resources.

## Phrase
**白手起家** — bái shǒu qǐ jiā
## Characters


白 (bái) — white, pure  
手 (shǒu) — hand  
起 (qǐ) — to rise, to start  
家 (jiā) — home, family, or business


## Origin
This idiom originates from the idea of someone who lacks any resources or inheritance but still manages to start a business and become successful through hard work and determination. It's often used to highlight the spirit of entrepreneurship and self-reliance in Chinese culture.

## Examples
- 他白手起家，建立了自己的公司。<br>He built his own company from scratch.
- 她白手起家，成为了亿万富翁。<br>She became a billionaire starting from nothing.
- 许多人都相信，白手起家是成功的关键。<br>Many people believe that starting from scratch is the key to success.

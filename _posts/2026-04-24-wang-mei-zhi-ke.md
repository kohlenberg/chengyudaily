> Ever found yourself craving something that’s just out of reach?

## Overview
Gazing at plums to quench thirst – a way to motivate oneself.

## Phrase
**望梅止渴** — wàng méi zhǐ kě
## Characters

望 (wàng) — to gaze  
梅 (méi) — plum  
止 (zhǐ) — to stop  
渴 (kě) — thirsty


## Origin
In ancient times, a weary traveler was feeling extremely thirsty. To ease his discomfort, he imagined a grove of plums nearby. Just thinking about the juicy fruit made his thirst feel more bearable and inspired him to keep going. This idiom reminds us that sometimes, envisioning a goal can help us push through challenges.

## Examples
- 他为了减肥，常常想象自己吃到美味的水果。<br>To lose weight, he often imagines himself eating delicious fruits.
- 学生们在考试期间，想象毕业后的美好生活。<br>During exams, students envision the wonderful life after graduation.
- 在工作压力大时，她会想着假期的旅行来放松自己。<br>When work pressure is high, she thinks about her vacation trip to relax herself.

> Today, we explore a saying that teaches us to think carefully before acting.

## Overview
To avoid harming the innocent while trying to catch the guilty.

## Phrase
**投鼠忌器** — tóu shǔ jì qì
## Characters


投 (tóu) — to throw  
鼠 (shǔ) — mouse  
忌 (jì) — to avoid or be cautious of  
器 (qì) — tool or instrument


## Origin
This idiom comes from a story about a cat trying to catch a mouse. The cat was worried that if it threw something to hit the mouse, it might break the pottery nearby. So, it held back, realizing that its actions could harm more than just the mouse. This reflects the idea of being mindful of the consequences of our actions.

## Examples
- 为了抓到小偷，他投鼠忌器，不想伤到无辜的人。<br>To catch the thief, he was cautious not to harm innocent people.
- 在处理这件事时，我们应该投鼠忌器，考虑后果。<br>When handling this matter, we should be careful to consider the consequences.
- 她在做决定时总是投鼠忌器，避免给别人带来麻烦。<br>She always avoids harm to others when making decisions.

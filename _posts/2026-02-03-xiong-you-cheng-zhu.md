Welcome to Chengyu Bites — your quick summary on Chinese 成语. Ever wondered how to express confidence in your plans? Let's dive into this idiom!

## Overview
Having a well-thought-out plan or preparation.

## Phrase
**胸有成竹** — xiōng yǒu chéng zhú
## Characters


胸 (xiōng) — chest, heart  
有 (yǒu) — to have  
成 (chéng) — to succeed, to complete  
竹 (zhú) — bamboo


## Origin
This idiom originates from a painter during the Song Dynasty who was known for his incredible skill in painting bamboo. He would visualize the bamboo in his mind before he even picked up the brush, showcasing his preparation and confidence. As a result, the phrase came to symbolize the importance of having a clear plan before taking action.

## Examples
- 他在考试前复习得很充分, 胸有成竹.<br>He reviewed thoroughly before the exam, feeling confident and well-prepared.
- 这个项目经理做了详细的计划, 所以他在会议上胸有成竹.<br>The project manager made detailed plans, so he was confident during the meeting.
- 她在演讲前准备了很久, 胸有成竹地走上了舞台.<br>She prepared for a long time before the speech, confidently stepping onto the stage.

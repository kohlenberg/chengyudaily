## Overview
Heard with ears and seen with eyes; firsthand experience.

## Phrase
**耳闻目睹** — ěr wén mù dǔ
## Characters


耳 (ěr) — ear  
闻 (wén) — hear  
目 (mù) — eye  
睹 (dǔ) — see


## Origin
This idiom comes from ancient times when people relied heavily on direct observation and auditory information. It emphasizes the importance of experiencing events personally rather than relying on hearsay. Many historical texts highlight the value of being an eyewitness to events, as it often leads to more credible accounts. Over time, this idiom became a staple in conversations about truth and reliability.

## Examples
- 你可以相信她的故事，因为她是耳闻目睹的。<br>You can trust her story because she witnessed it firsthand.
- 我耳闻目睹了整个事件，所以我能告诉你真相。<br>I saw the whole incident firsthand, so I can tell you the truth.
- 有些事情只有耳闻目睹才能理解。<br>Some things can only be understood through firsthand experience.

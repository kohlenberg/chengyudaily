Welcome to Chengyu Bites — your quick summary on Chinese 成语. Ever wondered about getting rewards without putting in the work? Let's dive into today's idiom!

## Overview
Gaining without effort.

## Phrase
**不劳而获** — bù láo ér huò
## Characters


不 (bù) — not  
劳 (láo) — labor  
而 (ér) — and  
获 (huò) — to gain


## Origin
This idiom originates from ancient Chinese texts where it was stated that one cannot expect to harvest crops without first toiling the land. It reflects the idea that effort is necessary to achieve success, and nothing worthwhile comes without hard work. Over time, this phrase has been used to caution against laziness and unrealistic expectations in life.

## Examples
- 他想不劳而获，结果失去了所有。<br>He wanted to gain without effort and ended up losing everything.
- 不要期望不劳而获，努力工作才是成功的关键。<br>Don't expect to gain without effort; hard work is the key to success.
- 这样的想法只会让你不劳而获。<br>Such thinking will only lead you to expect rewards without effort.

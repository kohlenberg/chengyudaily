## Overview
Remember the past to guide the future.

## Phrase
**前事不忘，后事之师** — qián shì bù wàng, hòu shì zhī shī
## Characters

前 (qián) — before  
事 (shì) — matter/event  
不 (bù) — not  
忘 (wàng) — forget  
后 (hòu) — after  
事 (shì) — matter/event  
之 (zhī) — of  
师 (shī) — teacher/guide


## Origin
This idiom emphasizes the importance of learning from past experiences. It originates from the idea that history serves as a valuable teacher. By reflecting on previous events, we can avoid making the same mistakes. Many renowned leaders and thinkers have echoed this wisdom throughout history.

## Examples
- 我们应该记住前事不忘，后事之师。<br>We should remember that past events guide our future.
- 她总是从错误中学习，前事不忘，后事之师。<br>She always learns from her mistakes; remembering the past guides her future.
- 这本书教导我们前事不忘，后事之师的重要性。<br>This book teaches us the importance of remembering the past to guide the future.

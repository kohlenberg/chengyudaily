## Overview
To extend one's own feelings to others; to empathize.

## Phrase
**推己及人** — tuī jǐ jí rén
## Characters


推 (tuī) — to push  
己 (jǐ) — oneself  
及 (jí) — to reach  
人 (rén) — people


## Origin
This idiom originates from the idea that one should treat others the way they wish to be treated themselves. It emphasizes the importance of empathy and understanding in human interactions. The concept is deeply rooted in Confucianism, which encourages individuals to consider the feelings and perspectives of others. Thus, 推己及人 serves as a moral reminder.

## Examples
- 他总是推己及人，关心朋友的感受。<br>He always empathizes and cares about his friends' feelings.
- 在工作中，推己及人能促进团队的合作。<br>Empathizing with others at work can enhance teamwork.
- 教育孩子推己及人是很重要的。<br>It is important to teach children to empathize with others.

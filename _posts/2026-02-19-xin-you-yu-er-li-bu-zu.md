Welcome to Chengyu Bites — your quick summary on Chinese 成语. Ever felt motivated but unable to act? This idiom captures that feeling perfectly. 

## Overview
The heart is willing, but the strength is insufficient.

## Phrase
**心有余而力不足** — xīn yǒu yú ér lì bù zú
## Characters


心 (xīn) — heart, mind  
有 (yǒu) — to have  
余 (yú) — surplus, excess  
而 (ér) — but  
力 (lì) — strength, power  
不足 (bù zú) — insufficient, lacking


## Origin
The idiom originates from a tale in ancient China where a man wanted to climb a mountain, driven by his enthusiasm and determination. However, despite his strong desire to reach the summit, his physical strength was not enough to carry him there. This contrast between aspiration and ability led to the saying we use today.

## Examples
- 他心有余而力不足，虽然想帮助朋友，但却无能为力。<br>He has the will to help his friend, but he is powerless to do so.
- 这位老年人心有余而力不足，想参加马拉松，但身体不允许。<br>This elderly person wants to participate in the marathon, but his body won’t allow it.
- 她心有余而力不足，想学新技能，但工作太忙。<br>She wants to learn new skills, but she's too busy with work.

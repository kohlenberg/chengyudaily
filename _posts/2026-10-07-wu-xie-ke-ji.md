> Today, we’re diving into a term that describes perfection in a unique way.

## Overview
flawless; without any loopholes

## Phrase
**无懈可击** — wú xiè kě jī
## Characters


无 (wú) — without  
懈 (xiè) — laziness/weakness  
可 (kě) — can/able to  
击 (jī) — hit/strike


## Origin
This idiom originates from a story about a skilled archer. The archer was so precise that no one could find any mistakes in his technique. No matter how hard others tried to criticize or point out errors, they simply couldn't. Thus, the phrase evolved to represent anything that is impeccable.

## Examples
- 他的计划无懈可击。<br>His plan is flawless.
- 她的演讲无懈可击。<br>Her speech was without any flaws.
- 这幅画无懈可击。<br>This painting is perfect.

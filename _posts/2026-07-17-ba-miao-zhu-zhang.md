## Overview
To pull seedlings to help them grow; to spoil things by excessive intervention.

## Phrase
**拔苗助长** — bá miáo zhù zhǎng
## Characters


拔 (bá) — pull out  
苗 (miáo) — seedling  
助 (zhù) — help  
长 (zhǎng) — grow


## Origin
In ancient China, a farmer was eager for a good harvest. He thought that if he pulled up the seedlings to help them grow faster, he would get a bigger yield. Unfortunately, his impatience led to the destruction of the plants, and he ended up with nothing. This story teaches us that rushing things can often lead to failure.

## Examples
- 他总是想拔苗助长，结果总是失败。<br>He always wants to rush things, and as a result, he always fails.
- 教孩子时，拔苗助长只会让他们变得更加焦虑。<br>When teaching children, pushing them too hard will only make them more anxious.
- 在工作中，过度干预可能会导致团队效率降低，拔苗助长。<br>In work, excessive intervention can lower team efficiency; it's like pulling seedlings to help them grow.

Today, we explore a phrase that reflects the shared grief of friends in peril.

## Overview
The death of a rabbit causes sorrow for a fox.

## Phrase
**兔死狐悲** — tù sǐ hú bēi
## Characters


兔 (tù) — rabbit  
死 (sǐ) — to die  
狐 (hú) — fox  
悲 (bēi) — to grieve


## Origin
In ancient times, a fox and a rabbit were close friends. One day, the rabbit fell into a trap and died. The fox grieved deeply, realizing that the rabbit's death was a warning of the dangers that could also befall him. This idiom emerged from that poignant moment, symbolizing the shared grief amongst friends facing similar threats.

## Examples
- 朋友的失业让我感到兔死狐悲。<br>My friend's unemployment makes me feel sorrowful about our shared struggles.
- 当我听到邻居的灾难，我感到兔死狐悲。<br>When I heard about my neighbor's disaster, I felt a sense of shared grief.
- 看到同事离职，我心中充满兔死狐悲。<br>Seeing a colleague leave filled me with sorrow for our shared experiences.

## Closing
Remember the idiom 兔死狐悲, which means to feel sorrow for someone else's misfortune.

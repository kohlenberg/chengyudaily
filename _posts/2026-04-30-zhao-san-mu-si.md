## Overview
Changing one's mind frequently; inconsistent.

## Phrase
**朝三暮四** — zhāo sān mù sì
## Characters


朝 (zhāo) — morning  
三 (sān) — three  
暮 (mù) — evening  
四 (sì) — four


## Origin
This idiom originates from a fable about a man who fed his monkeys. He promised them three nuts in the morning and four at night. When he changed his mind to four in the morning and three at night, the monkeys became upset. This illustrates how people can be easily dissatisfied with change, especially when it's unexpected.

## Examples
- 他总是朝三暮四，根本不可靠。<br>He is always inconsistent; he is simply unreliable.
- 政策的变化让大家朝三暮四。<br>The changes in policy have made everyone inconsistent.
- 她的态度朝三暮四，我不知道该相信什么。<br>Her attitude is so fickle; I don’t know what to believe.

## Overview
Hearts linked together; a deep mutual understanding.

## Phrase
**心心相印** — xīn xīn xiāng yìn
## Characters


心 (xīn) — heart  
心 (xīn) — heart  
相 (xiāng) — mutual / each other  
印 (yìn) — to stamp / to mark


## Origin
This idiom originates from a story in ancient China, where two lovers, despite being separated, could communicate their feelings through their hearts' connection. They understood each other's thoughts and emotions without needing words. This bond was so strong that it was said their hearts were in perfect harmony.

## Examples
- 他们在一起的时候，心心相印。<br>When they're together, their hearts are in perfect harmony.
- 这对朋友之间心心相印，默契极了。<br>There is a deep mutual understanding between these friends; they are in perfect sync.
- 他们的爱情故事让人感到心心相印。<br>Their love story makes one feel a deep connection.

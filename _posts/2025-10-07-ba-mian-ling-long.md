## Overview
Versatile and adept at handling various situations.

## Phrase
**八面玲珑** — bā miàn líng lóng
## Characters


八 (bā) — eight  
面 (miàn) — sides or faces  
玲 (líng) — tinkling or clever  
决 (lóng) — delicate or exquisite


## Origin
This idiom originates from the description of a skilled artisan who could create a beautiful, multi-faceted piece of jade, reflecting light from all angles. Just like that jade, a person who is 八面玲珑 can navigate social situations with ease and charm, making them well-liked in various circles. It emphasizes the ability to adapt and connect with different people effortlessly.

## Examples
- 他在公司里八面玲珑，大家都很喜欢他。<br>He is versatile at the company, and everyone likes him.
- 她的社交技巧真是八面玲珑。<br>Her social skills are truly versatile.
- 在这个行业，八面玲珑的人更容易成功。<br>In this industry, versatile people are more likely to succeed.

## Overview
A quiet person surprises everyone when they finally speak.

## Phrase
**不鸣则已，一鸣惊人** — bù míng zé yǐ, yī míng jīng rén
## Characters


不 (bù) — not  
鸣 (míng) — to chirp or make a sound  
则 (zé) — then  
已 (yǐ) — to cease  
一 (yī) — one  
鸣 (míng) — to chirp or make a sound  
惊 (jīng) — to surprise  
人 (rén) — person


## Origin
This idiom originates from a fable about a talented bird that remained silent for a long time. Once it finally sang, its beautiful voice amazed everyone around. This story emphasizes that sometimes, those who are quiet have much to offer, and when they finally share their gifts, it can be truly remarkable.

## Examples
- 他平时不说话，但考试时表现得非常好。<br>He usually doesn't speak, but performs exceptionally well during exams.
- 她一直在为比赛练习，结果一上场就赢了。<br>She practiced for the competition quietly, and when she performed, she won immediately.
- 这位作家多年没有发表作品，最近的新书让大家惊讶。<br>This author hadn't published anything for years, and their recent book surprised everyone.

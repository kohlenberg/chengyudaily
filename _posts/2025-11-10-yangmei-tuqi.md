> Today, we explore a phrase that embodies pride and relief.

## Overview
To raise one's eyebrows and exhale, meaning to feel proud and relieved.

## Phrase
**扬眉吐气** — yángméi tǔqì
## Characters


扬 (yáng) — to raise  
眉 (méi) — eyebrows  
吐 (tǔ) — to exhale  
气 (qì) — breath


## Origin
This idiom originates from the feeling of triumph after overcoming challenges, where one can finally lift their eyebrows and breathe out a sigh of relief. It often describes moments when someone feels vindicated or successful after a struggle. The image of raising eyebrows symbolizes confidence and satisfaction.

## Examples
- 他通过了考试，终于扬眉吐气。<br>He passed the exam and finally feels proud and relieved.
- 她在比赛中获胜，扬眉吐气。<br>She won the competition and felt proud and relieved.
- 经过多年的努力，他终于扬眉吐气。<br>After years of hard work, he finally feels proud and relieved.

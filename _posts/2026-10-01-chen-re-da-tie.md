Ever heard the phrase that encourages you to take action at the right time?

## Overview
Strike while the iron is hot; seize the moment.

## Phrase
**趁热打铁** — chèn rè dǎ tiě
## Characters


趁 (chèn) — take advantage of  
热 (rè) — hot  
打 (dǎ) — strike  
铁 (tiě) — iron


## Origin
This idiom comes from the ancient practice of blacksmithing. When iron is heated, it becomes malleable and easier to shape. If a blacksmith waits too long to strike, the iron cools and hardens, making it difficult to work with. Thus, it teaches us the importance of acting promptly and taking advantage of favorable conditions.

## Examples
- 赶快做决定，趁热打铁！<br>Make the decision quickly, strike while the iron is hot!
- 我们要趁热打铁，抓住这个机会。<br>We need to seize the moment and grab this opportunity.
- 他建议我们趁热打铁，尽快完成项目。<br>He suggested that we act swiftly and finish the project quickly.

## Overview
A small stream flowing for a long time; sustainable resources.

## Phrase
**细水长流** — xì shuǐ cháng liú
## Characters

细 (xì) — fine, thin  
水 (shuǐ) — water  
长 (cháng) — long  
流 (liú) — flow


## Origin
This idiom originates from ancient Chinese poetry, where it was observed that a small stream could nourish a vast land over time. Instead of relying on a sudden downpour, the slow and steady flow of water was seen as more reliable and beneficial. This concept highlights the importance of patience and consistency in achieving long-term success.

## Examples
- 他每天存一点钱，真是细水长流。<br>He saves a little money every day; it's truly a case of 'little streams flowing long.'
- 只要坚持练习，细水长流，总会有进步。<br>As long as you keep practicing, little by little, you will definitely improve over time.
- 教育是细水长流的过程，需要耐心。<br>Education is a long-term process that requires patience, just like a small stream flowing steadily.

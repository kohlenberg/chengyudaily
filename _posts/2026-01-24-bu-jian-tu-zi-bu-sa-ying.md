Welcome to Chengyu Bites — your quick summary on Chinese 成语. Ever wondered about the patience required in achieving success? This idiom has the answer. The phrase is: 不见兔子不撒鹰, bù jiàn tù zǐ bù sā yīng.

## Overview
You won't catch the eagle until you see the rabbit.

## Phrase
**不见兔子不撒鹰** — bù jiàn tù zǐ bù sā yīng
## Characters


不 (bù) — not  
见 (jiàn) — see  
兔子 (tù zǐ) — rabbit  
不 (bù) — not  
撒 (sā) — release  
鹰 (yīng) — eagle


## Origin
In ancient times, hunters would set their sights on catching rabbits using trained eagles. However, they knew that they couldn't let the eagle fly until they spotted a rabbit; otherwise, it would be a waste of effort. This idiom reflects the idea of waiting for the right moment before taking action, emphasizing patience and strategy in achieving goals.

## Examples
- 你在做这个项目之前，不见兔子不撒鹰。<br>You shouldn't proceed with this project until you see clear evidence of its viability.
- 在投资之前，不见兔子不撒鹰，先做市场调研。<br>Before investing, wait for clear signs and conduct market research first.
- 他总是说要等到有机会才行动，不见兔子不撒鹰。<br>He always says he will act only when there’s an opportunity; patience is key.

## Closing

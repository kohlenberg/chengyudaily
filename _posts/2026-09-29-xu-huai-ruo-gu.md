> Today, we explore the beauty of humility in a powerful idiom.

## Overview
Humble and open-minded, like a valley.

## Phrase
**虚怀若谷** — xū huái ruò gǔ
## Characters


虚 (xū) — empty, void  
怀 (huái) — heart, mind  
若 (ruò) — like, as if  
谷 (gǔ) — valley


## Origin
In ancient times, a wise teacher was known for his vast knowledge yet remained very humble. One day, a student asked him how he could be so knowledgeable. The teacher pointed to a deep valley and said, "Just as this valley is open to receive all the rain, we too must be open to learn from everyone around us." This wisdom became the foundation of the idiom.

## Examples
- 他总是虚怀若谷，愿意听取别人的意见。<br>He is always humble and willing to listen to others' opinions.
- 虚怀若谷是成功人士的重要特质。<br>Humility is an important trait of successful people.
- 我们应该虚怀若谷，学习他人的优点。<br>We should be humble and learn from the strengths of others.

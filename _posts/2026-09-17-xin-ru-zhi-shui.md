## Overview
Heart like still water; calm and composed.

## Phrase
**心如止水** — xīn rú zhǐ shuǐ
## Characters


心 (xīn) — heart  
如 (rú) — like  
止 (zhǐ) — still  
水 (shuǐ) — water


## Origin
This idiom originates from ancient texts, where a calm heart is likened to still water reflecting the tranquility of nature. It emphasizes the importance of maintaining inner peace amidst chaos. The phrase encourages a serene mindset, allowing clarity in decision-making and emotional stability.

## Examples
- 在比赛中，他的心如止水，冷静地做出决策。<br>During the competition, his heart was like still water, calmly making decisions.
- 面对压力，她总能保持心如止水的状态。<br>Faced with pressure, she always manages to maintain a state of calm.
- 在困难时刻，他的心如止水，帮助团队渡过难关。<br>In difficult moments, his heart was like still water, helping the team overcome challenges.

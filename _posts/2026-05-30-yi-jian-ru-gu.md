> Ever met someone and felt like you've known them forever? This idiom captures that feeling.

## Overview
Literally 'at first sight like old friends'; figuratively means to feel an instant connection.

## Phrase
**一见如故** — yī jiàn rú gù
## Characters


一 (yī) — one  
见 (jiàn) — see  
如 (rú) — like  
故 (gù) — old friend


## Origin
This idiom dates back to the Tang Dynasty, where it was said that two people who met for the first time felt as if they had been friends for years. It highlighted the warmth and ease in their interaction, symbolizing that some connections are simply meant to be. The phrase has since been used to describe those serendipitous encounters that leave a lasting impression.

## Examples
- 他们第一次见面就一见如故。<br>They felt like old friends when they met for the first time.
- 我和她一见如故，聊了很久。<br>She and I hit it off right away and talked for a long time.
- 这两位艺术家在展览上相遇，立刻一见如故。<br>The two artists met at the exhibition and instantly felt like old friends.

## Closing
一见如故 means to feel an instant connection.

Ever heard someone say 'that's just 鸡毛蒜皮'? Let's explore this colorful idiom together.

## Overview
Trivial matters or insignificant details.

## Phrase
**鸡毛蒜皮** — jī máo suàn pí
## Characters


鸡 (jī) — chicken  
毛 (máo) — feather  
蒜 (suàn) — garlic  
皮 (pí) — skin


## Origin
In ancient times, people would often engage in debates about the importance of various items. One day, a philosopher observed that people were wasting time arguing over things as inconsequential as chicken feathers and garlic skins. He used this observation to warn others about the dangers of focusing on trivial matters instead of more meaningful issues.

## Examples
- 这件事完全是鸡毛蒜皮。<br>This matter is completely trivial.
- 不要为这些鸡毛蒜皮的事情争论。<br>Don't argue over these insignificant issues.
- 他的抱怨都是鸡毛蒜皮。<br>His complaints are all about trivial matters.

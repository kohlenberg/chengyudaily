Welcome to Chengyu Bites — your quick summary on Chinese 成语. Ever wondered how a tiger can become even more formidable? Let's find out! The phrase is: 如虎添翼, rú hǔ tiān yì.

## Overview
Adding wings to a tiger; giving someone or something a powerful boost.

## Phrase
**如虎添翼** — rú hǔ tiān yì
## Characters


如 (rú) — like, as  
虎 (hǔ) — tiger  
添 (tiān) — to add  
翼 (yì) — wings


## Origin
This idiom originates from ancient Chinese literature, where tigers were seen as powerful and fierce creatures. The concept of adding wings to such a creature symbolizes enhancing its strength and ability. It suggests that when a strong force receives additional support, it becomes even more unstoppable. This imagery has resonated through the ages, reminding us of the power of synergy.

## Examples
- 他在比赛中如虎添翼，轻松赢得了冠军。<br>He was like a tiger with wings in the competition, easily winning the championship.
- 新的科技让公司的发展如虎添翼。<br>The new technology has given the company's growth a powerful boost.
- 有了这些支持，他们的计划如虎添翼。<br>With this support, their plans are like a tiger with wings.

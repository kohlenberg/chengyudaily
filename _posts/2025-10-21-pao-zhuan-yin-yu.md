Welcome to Chengyu Bites — your quick summary on Chinese 成语. Ever wondered how a small gesture can lead to great rewards? The phrase is: 抛砖引玉, pāo zhuān yǐn yù.

## Overview
Throwing out a brick to attract jade (offering something of lesser value to gain something more valuable).

## Phrase
**抛砖引玉** — pāo zhuān yǐn yù
## Characters


抛 (pāo) — to throw  
砖 (zhuān) — brick  
引 (yǐn) — to attract  
玉 (yù) — jade


## Origin
This idiom originates from a story where a scholar used a simple stone to entice talented individuals to share their knowledge. By presenting a lesser gift, he encouraged others to offer their greater insights in return. This clever tactic highlighted the value of humility and the wisdom that can come from collaboration.

## Examples
- 他在会议上抛砖引玉，希望大家提出更好的建议。<br>He threw out a simple idea at the meeting, hoping everyone would suggest better ones.
- 她用自己的经历抛砖引玉，鼓励其他人分享故事。<br>She shared her own experience to attract others to share their stories.
- 通过抛砖引玉，他成功吸引了更多投资者的关注。<br>By throwing out a small proposal, he successfully attracted more investors' attention.

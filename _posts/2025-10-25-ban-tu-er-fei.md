## Overview
To give up halfway; to abandon a task before completing it.

## Phrase
**半途而废** — bàn tú ér fèi
## Characters


半 (bàn) — half  
途 (tú) — way  
而 (ér) — and  
废 (fèi) — to give up


## Origin
This idiom originates from ancient Chinese literature, where it was used to describe someone who starts a journey or task with enthusiasm but loses motivation midway. It highlights the frustration of seeing potential wasted and emphasizes the importance of seeing things through to the end.

## Examples
- 他做事情总是半途而废。<br>He always gives up halfway in everything he does.
- 如果你半途而废，就永远无法成功。<br>If you give up halfway, you will never succeed.
- 学习需要坚持，不能半途而废。<br>Learning requires persistence; you can't give up halfway.

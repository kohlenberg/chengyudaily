## Overview
To seek fish by climbing a tree; pursuing the impossible.

## Phrase
**缘木求鱼** — yuán mù qiú yú
## Characters


缘 (yuán) — to be related or connected  
木 (mù) — tree  
求 (qiú) — to seek or ask for  
鱼 (yú) — fish


## Origin
This idiom originates from a story in ancient China where someone tried to catch fish by climbing a tree, which is obviously futile. It highlights the absurdity of trying to achieve something that cannot be done, emphasizing that the approach must match the goal. This phrase serves as a reminder to use the right methods to achieve our objectives.

## Examples
- 他想通过赌博致富，真是缘木求鱼。<br>He thinks he can get rich by gambling; that's truly seeking fish by climbing a tree.
- 如果你不学习，却想考试得高分，那就是缘木求鱼。<br>If you don’t study but want to score high on the exam, that’s just seeking fish by climbing a tree.
- 他们试图通过不合法的手段获得成功，完全是缘木求鱼。<br>They are trying to achieve success through illegal means, which is completely seeking fish by climbing a tree.

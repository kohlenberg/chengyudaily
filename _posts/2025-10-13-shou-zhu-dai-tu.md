Have you ever waited for luck to strike without taking action?

## Overview
Waiting idly for opportunities to come without effort.

## Phrase
**守株待兔** — shǒu zhū dài tù
## Characters


守 (shǒu) — to guard or keep  
株 (zhū) — a tree stump  
待 (dài) — to wait  
兔 (tù) — rabbit


## Origin
In ancient times, a farmer saw a rabbit accidentally kill itself by crashing into a tree stump. Excited by this stroke of luck, he decided to stop working in his fields and instead waited by the stump for more rabbits to come. Days passed, but no more rabbits appeared, and his fields became overgrown. This story serves as a warning against relying solely on luck.

## Examples
- 他总是守株待兔，不愿意主动寻找工作。<br>He always waits idly for opportunities and is unwilling to actively search for a job.
- 这种守株待兔的态度不会带来成功。<br>This kind of idle waiting will not bring success.
- 如果你守株待兔，就会错过很多机会。<br>If you wait idly for luck, you will miss many opportunities.

## Closing
守株待兔 reminds us that relying on luck alone is not the way to achieve our goals.

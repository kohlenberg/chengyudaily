## Overview
A journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step.

## Phrase
**千里之行，始于足下** — qiān lǐ zhī xíng, shǐ yú zú xià
## Characters


千 (qiān) — thousand  
里 (lǐ) — miles  
之 (zhī) — 'of' or possessive particle  
行 (xíng) — journey or to walk  
始 (shǐ) — to begin  
于 (yú) — at or in  
足 (zú) — foot  
下 (xià) — below or under


## Origin
This idiom originates from the Dao De Jing, a foundational text of Daoism written by Laozi. It highlights the notion that no matter how grand a goal might be, progress starts with small, manageable actions. This wisdom has inspired many to pursue their dreams, reminding us that every big achievement begins with a single step.

## Examples
- 做事情要从小做起，千里之行，始于足下。<br>You should start small when doing things; a journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step.
- 学习新技能时，千里之行，始于足下。<br>When learning a new skill, a journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step.
- 他明白了，千里之行，始于足下，决定从今天开始锻炼。<br>He understood that a journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step and decided to start exercising today.

## Closing
千里之行，始于足下 - a journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step.

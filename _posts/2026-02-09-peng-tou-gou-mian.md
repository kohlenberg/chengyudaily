## Overview
disheveled and unkempt; looking messy

## Phrase
**蓬头垢面** — péng tóu gòu miàn
## Characters


蓬 (péng) — unkempt or tangled  
头 (tóu) — head  
垢 (gòu) — dirt or grime  
面 (miàn) — face


## Origin
In ancient China, individuals who were too focused on their studies or work often neglected their personal grooming. As a result, they would appear disheveled, with messy hair and a dirty face. This idiom reflects that state of being so absorbed in one's pursuits that self-care is overlooked.

## Examples
- 他最近一直忙于工作，整个人看起来蓬头垢面。<br>He has been so busy with work lately that he looks disheveled and unkempt.
- 她没有时间打理自己，看上去蓬头垢面。<br>She hasn't had time to take care of herself and looks messy.
- 在比赛前，他太紧张了，根本顾不上自己的形象，结果蓬头垢面。<br>Before the competition, he was so nervous that he forgot about his appearance and ended up looking unkempt.

## Overview
To stir up trouble and alert the enemy.

## Phrase
**打草惊蛇** — dǎ cǎo jīng shé
## Characters


打 (dǎ) — to hit  
草 (cǎo) — grass  
惊 (jīng) — to scare  
蛇 (shé) — snake


## Origin
In ancient times, a hunter was trying to catch a snake hiding in the grass. He started hitting the grass, thinking it would flush the snake out. Instead, the noise scared the snake away, teaching him that sometimes, revealing your plans can lead to failure. This led to the saying that emphasizes caution in how one approaches a situation.

## Examples
- 他打草惊蛇，结果计划失败了。<br>He stirred up trouble and ended up failing his plan.
- 你不应该告诉太多人，这样会打草惊蛇。<br>You shouldn't tell too many people; it will alert the enemy.
- 打草惊蛇之后，敌人开始警觉。<br>After stirring up trouble, the enemy became alert.

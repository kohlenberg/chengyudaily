## Overview
To be blindly arrogant or overestimate oneself.

## Phrase
**夜郎自大** — Yèláng zì dà
## Characters


夜 (Yè) — night  
郎 (láng) — a young man or a person  
自 (zì) — self  
大 (dà) — big or great


## Origin
The idiom originates from a historical account about a small kingdom called Yelang, located in what is now southwestern China. The king of Yelang, believing his kingdom was the most powerful, refused to acknowledge the existence of larger and more influential states around him. This arrogance eventually led to the downfall of his kingdom when stronger forces challenged him.

## Examples
- 他总是认为自己是公司里最聪明的，真是夜郎自大。<br>He always thinks he is the smartest in the company; he is truly blindly arrogant.
- 她在比赛中表现得夜郎自大，结果输了。<br>She acted arrogantly in the competition and ended up losing.
- 有些人对自己的能力夜郎自大，结果在工作中犯了很多错误。<br>Some people are blindly arrogant about their abilities and end up making many mistakes at work.

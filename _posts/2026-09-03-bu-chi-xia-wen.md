## Overview
Not ashamed to ask those below you; humility in seeking knowledge.

## Phrase
**不耻下问** — bù chǐ xià wèn
## Characters


不 (bù) — not  
耻 (chǐ) — ashamed  
下 (xià) — below  
问 (wèn) — to ask


## Origin
This idiom comes from the teachings of Confucius, who emphasized the importance of learning from anyone, regardless of their social standing. He often encouraged his students to ask questions, even to those considered less knowledgeable. The idea was that true wisdom comes from being open and willing to learn.

## Examples
- 他不耻下问，向老师请教问题。<br>He is not ashamed to ask questions of his teacher.
- 在工作中，年轻人应该不耻下问，向前辈学习。<br>In the workplace, young people should not be ashamed to ask and learn from their seniors.
- 她总是鼓励学生不耻下问，追求知识。<br>She always encourages students to be humble and seek knowledge.

Welcome to Chengyu Bites — your quick summary on Chinese 成语. Ever wished for something but didn't take the leap to get it? 

## Overview
To admire fish in the water without taking action; wanting something without effort.

## Phrase
**临渊羡鱼** — lín yuān xiàn yú
## Characters


临 (lín) — to be by  
渊 (yuān) — deep water  
羡 (xiàn) — to admire  
鱼 (yú) — fish


## Origin
This idiom comes from ancient times when people often saw fish swimming in deep waters. Many admired them from the shore, wishing to catch them, yet they never took the plunge. It highlights the idea that simply wishing for something without taking action will lead to unfulfilled desires.

## Examples
- 他总是临渊羡鱼，不愿意努力工作。<br>He always admires the fish in the water but is unwilling to work hard.
- 她在网上看到很多美食，却只是临渊羡鱼，没有尝试做。<br>She saw a lot of delicious food online but only admired it without trying to make it.
- 如果你想成功，就不能临渊羡鱼。<br>If you want to succeed, you can't just admire it from a distance.

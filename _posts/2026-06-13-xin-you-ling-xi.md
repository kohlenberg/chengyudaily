## Overview
literally 'the heart has a spiritual connection'; figuratively, it means a deep understanding between people.

## Phrase
**心有灵犀** — xīn yǒu líng xī
## Characters


心 (xīn) — heart  
有 (yǒu) — has  
灵 (líng) — spiritual  
犀 (xī) — rhinoceros


## Origin
The idiom originates from a poem in ancient China, where a poet expressed the bond between two souls that could understand each other without words. It describes how a rhinoceros's horn, known for its magical qualities, symbolizes this rare connection. The two individuals share thoughts and feelings so deeply that they can almost communicate telepathically. This notion of silent understanding has resonated through the ages.

## Examples
- 他们之间心有灵犀，常常不需要多说什么。<br>There’s a deep understanding between them; they often don’t need to say much.
- 我和我的好朋友心有灵犀，心里想的事情她总能猜到。<br>My best friend and I have a spiritual connection; she can always guess what I'm thinking.
- 这种默契让他们的合作非常顺利，因为心有灵犀。<br>This tacit understanding makes their collaboration very smooth because they have that deep connection.

## Overview
To rise again after a setback.

## Phrase
**东山再起** — dōng shān zài qǐ
## Characters


东 (dōng) — east  
山 (shān) — mountain  
再 (zài) — again  
起 (qǐ) — to rise


## Origin
This idiom originates from the story of a famous general during the Southern Dynasties. After being defeated, he retreated to the East Mountain. Years later, he made a successful return to power, symbolizing resilience and the ability to start anew.

## Examples
- 他经历了很多困难，但他总是东山再起。<br>He has gone through many difficulties, but he always manages to make a comeback.
- 这位企业家在破产后东山再起，重新建立了自己的公司。<br>This entrepreneur rose again after bankruptcy and rebuilt his company.
- 她在比赛中失利，但东山再起，明年会再参加。<br>She lost in the competition, but she'll rise again and participate next year.

## Closing
东山再起 means to rise again after a setback.

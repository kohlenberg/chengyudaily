Today, we explore the essence of selflessness in leadership.

## Overview
Impartial and selfless; putting the public's interests first.

## Phrase
**大公无私** — dà gōng wú sī
## Characters


大 (dà) — big or great  
公 (gōng) — public or fair  
无 (wú) — without or no  
私 (sī) — self or private


## Origin
This idiom stems from ancient Chinese governance, where leaders were expected to prioritize the welfare of the people over personal gain. It highlights the ideal conduct of officials who serve selflessly, ensuring fairness and justice. The concept is deeply ingrained in Confucian values, promoting the notion that true leadership is marked by public spirit.

## Examples
- 这个领导者真是大公无私。<br>This leader is truly selfless.
- 在这个项目中，我们要大公无私，公平对待所有人。<br>In this project, we need to be impartial and treat everyone fairly.
- 大公无私是优秀领导者必备的品质。<br>Selflessness is an essential quality of an excellent leader.

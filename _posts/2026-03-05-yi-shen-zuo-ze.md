## Overview
Lead by example; set a good example through one's own actions.

## Phrase
**以身作则** — yǐ shēn zuò zé
## Characters


以 (yǐ) — to use  
身 (shēn) — body  
作 (zuò) — to do / to make  
则 (zé) — rule / standard


## Origin
This idiom originates from the idea that leaders must set a good example for their followers. It emphasizes the importance of demonstrating the behavior you wish to see in others. In ancient China, it was believed that a ruler's virtue would inspire the people to follow suit. Thus, leading by personal example became a key principle in governance and education.

## Examples
- 老师说，作为学生，我们应该以身作则。<br>The teacher said that as students, we should lead by example.
- 经理以身作则，大家都愿意跟随他的领导。<br>The manager leads by example, and everyone is willing to follow his leadership.
- 在家庭中，父母应以身作则，教导孩子。<br>In a family, parents should lead by example in teaching their children.

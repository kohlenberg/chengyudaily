> Today, we explore how stories and information spread through conversations.

## Overview
Passed down by word of mouth.

## Phrase
**口口相传** — kǒu kǒu xiāng chuán
## Characters


口 (kǒu) — mouth  
口 (kǒu) — mouth  
相 (xiāng) — each other  
传 (chuán) — to pass on


## Origin
The idiom "口口相传" emphasizes the importance of oral communication in sharing stories and information. It reflects a cultural practice where knowledge is transmitted through conversations rather than written texts. This method of passing down information has been crucial in preserving traditions and histories. The phrase highlights the reliability and intimacy of personal storytelling.

## Examples
- 这个故事是口口相传的。<br>This story has been passed down by word of mouth.
- 许多古老的传说都是通过口口相传流传下来的。<br>Many ancient legends have been passed down through conversations.
- 在这个小村庄里，历史是口口相传的。<br>In this small village, history is shared through word of mouth.

## Closing
口口相传 — a reminder of how stories are shared and preserved through conversation.

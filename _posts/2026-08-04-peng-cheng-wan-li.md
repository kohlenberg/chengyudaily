## Overview
A great journey ahead; vast potential.

## Phrase
**鹏程万里** — péng chéng wàn lǐ
## Characters


鹏 (péng) — a mythical bird  
程 (chéng) — journey or distance  
万 (wàn) — ten thousand, a large number  
里 (lǐ) — a unit of distance


## Origin
The idiom originates from the ancient text Zhuangzi, where a giant bird called the Peng flies high in the sky, traversing great distances. This imagery evokes the idea of pursuing one's dreams and ambitions without limits. The Peng represents not just any journey but an extraordinary one, inspiring those who hear it to aspire for greatness.

## Examples
- 他在事业上鹏程万里<br>He has a bright future in his career.
- 这位年轻的作家鹏程万里<br>This young writer has immense potential.
- 她的计划真是鹏程万里<br>Her plans are truly ambitious.

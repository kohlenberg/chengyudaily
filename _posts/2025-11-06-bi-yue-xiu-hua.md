> Today, we explore a phrase that captures the essence of beauty in nature and beyond.

## Overview
Literally: 'to close the moon and shame the flowers'; figuratively: describes a woman's beauty that surpasses nature.

## Phrase
**闭月羞花** — bì yuè xiū huā
## Characters


闭 (bì) — to close  
月 (yuè) — moon  
羞 (xiū) — to shame  
花 (huā) — flower


## Origin
This idiom originates from ancient Chinese literature, where legends often depicted beautiful women whose beauty was so stunning that it could overshadow the moon and make flowers feel ashamed of their own appearance. One famous example is the beauty of the legendary figure Xi Shi, known for her mesmerizing looks. Her beauty was said to be so extraordinary that even nature seemed to pale in comparison.

## Examples
- 她的美丽让人闭月羞花。<br>Her beauty makes the moon close and the flowers ashamed.
- 这位舞者真是闭月羞花。<br>This dancer is truly stunning, surpassing even the beauty of nature.
- 看到她，我觉得自己也闭月羞花。<br>Seeing her, I feel like I am outshone by her beauty.

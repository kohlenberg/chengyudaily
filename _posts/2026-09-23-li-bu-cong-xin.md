Ever felt like your ambitions were just out of reach? Let's explore that feeling in today’s idiom.

## Overview
Unable to act according to one's desires.

## Phrase
**力不从心** — lì bù cóng xīn
## Characters

力 (lì) — strength or power  
不 (bù) — not  
从 (cóng) — to follow  
心 (xīn) — heart or mind


## Origin
This idiom originates from the idea that sometimes, even if we have the desire and passion to do something, our physical strength or circumstances may not allow us to achieve it. It reflects a universal feeling of frustration when we encounter limitations that hinder our goals. This phrase resonates deeply in a fast-paced world where ambitions often exceed our capabilities.

## Examples
- 我想跑得很快，但我的腿力不从心。<br>I want to run fast, but my legs can't keep up with my heart's desire.
- 她的工作太多了，力不从心，感觉压力很大。<br>She has too much work, unable to keep up, feeling very stressed.
- 尽管他努力学习，但考试的结果还是让他力不从心。<br>Even though he studies hard, the exam results still make him feel powerless.

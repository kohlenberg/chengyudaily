## Overview
Rather die than be defeated.

## Phrase
**宁死不屈** — níng sǐ bù qū
## Characters


宁 (níng) — rather  
死 (sǐ) — die  
不 (bù) — not  
屈 (qū) — yield/bend


## Origin
This idiom originates from the historical context of Chinese resistance against invaders. It reflects the courage of individuals who would rather sacrifice their lives than submit to tyranny. Such unwavering spirit has been celebrated in literature and history, symbolizing the fight for freedom and dignity.

## Examples
- 他宁死不屈，决不妥协。<br>He would rather die than yield, and will never compromise.
- 在艰难时刻，她表现出宁死不屈的决心。<br>In difficult times, she showed a determination to never yield.
- 宁死不屈的精神激励着每一个战士。<br>The spirit of 'rather die than yield' inspires every soldier.

## Overview
A strong start with a weak finish.

## Phrase
**虎头蛇尾** — hǔ tóu shé wěi
## Characters


虎 (hǔ) — tiger  
头 (tóu) — head  
蛇 (shé) — snake  
尾 (wěi) — tail


## Origin
The idiom originates from a tale where a man carved a beautiful tiger's head but neglected to finish the tail, resulting in an incomplete work that looked odd. This illustrates how important it is to maintain consistency from beginning to end. The visual imagery of a powerful tiger head leading to a weak snake tail perfectly encapsulates this idea.

## Examples
- 他开始时很努力，但最后的报告却虎头蛇尾。<br>He worked very hard at first, but the final report ended up being a weak finish.
- 这个项目一开始很有潜力，结果却虎头蛇尾。<br>This project had great potential at the beginning, but it ended up fizzling out.
- 她的演讲虎头蛇尾，让人失望。<br>Her speech started strong but ended up being disappointing.

## Overview
As fish gain water; feeling at home or in one's element.

## Phrase
**如鱼得水** — rú yú dé shuǐ
## Characters

如 (rú) — as, like  
鱼 (yú) — fish  
得 (dé) — to obtain, to gain  
水 (shuǐ) — water


## Origin
In ancient China, fish need water to survive and thrive. When they are in water, they flourish and are free to swim effortlessly. Thus, the phrase emerged to describe someone who is in a situation that suits them perfectly, just as a fish is in water. This idiom emphasizes comfort and ease in one's environment.

## Examples
- 他在这家公司工作得如鱼得水。<br>He works in this company like a fish in water.
- 她在舞台上表演时，感觉如鱼得水。<br>She feels at home on stage, like a fish in water.
- 这个环境对他来说，简直是如鱼得水。<br>This environment is truly like a fish in water for him.

> Today, we're exploring a proverb that warns against rushing things.

## Overview
Haste makes waste.

## Phrase
**欲速则不达** — yù sù zé bù dá
## Characters


欲 (yù) — desire  
速 (sù) — quick  
则 (zé) — then  
不 (bù) — not  
达 (dá) — reach


## Origin
The phrase comes from ancient Chinese philosophy, emphasizing the importance of patience and careful planning. It suggests that when one tries to achieve something too quickly, they may end up making mistakes or missing their target altogether. This wisdom has been passed down through generations, reminding us that a steady pace often leads to better results.

## Examples
- 你太着急了，欲速则不达。<br>You're too anxious; haste makes waste.
- 在学习过程中，欲速则不达，慢慢来吧。<br>In the learning process, don't rush; take your time.
- 项目需要时间，欲速则不达。<br>The project needs time; haste makes waste.

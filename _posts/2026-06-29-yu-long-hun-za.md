## Overview
A mix of good and bad characters.

## Phrase
**鱼龙混杂** — yú lóng hùn zá
## Characters


鱼 (yú) — fish  
龙 (lóng) — dragon  
混 (hùn) — to mix  
杂 (zá) — to be mixed or mixed together


## Origin
In ancient times, the fish was seen as a common creature, while the dragon was a symbol of power and nobility. When these two were said to be mixed together, it indicated a confusing situation where high and low, good and bad, were all thrown into one place. This idiom reminds us to be cautious in our judgments, as appearances can be deceiving.

## Examples
- 这个市场里鱼龙混杂，买东西要小心。<br>This market is a mix of good and bad, so be careful when buying things.
- 在这个社交圈，鱼龙混杂，难以分辨谁是真正的朋友。<br>In this social circle, it's hard to distinguish who your true friends are because of the mix of characters.
- 他的演讲吸引了鱼龙混杂的听众。<br>His speech attracted a mixed audience of various types of people.

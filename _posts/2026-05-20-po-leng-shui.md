## Overview
To pour cold water on someone; to dampen enthusiasm.

## Phrase
**泼冷水** — pō lěng shuǐ
## Characters


泼 (pō) — to splash; to pour  
冷 (lěng) — cold  
水 (shuǐ) — water


## Origin
Imagine a hot summer day when someone is excited about going for a swim. Suddenly, someone splashes cold water on them, ruining the mood. This idiom conveys how negativity can dampen someone’s excitement or plans, much like that unexpected splash. It reminds us to be mindful of how our words can affect others.

## Examples
- 你总是泼冷水，别让大家失去信心！<br>You always pour cold water on things; don’t let everyone lose confidence!
- 她的评论真是泼冷水，让我很沮丧。<br>Her comment was really cold water; it made me very frustrated.
- 在这个项目上，请不要泼冷水，我们需要积极的反馈。<br>On this project, please don’t pour cold water; we need positive feedback.

## Overview
One word is worth nine tripods; a promise is invaluable.

## Phrase
**一言九鼎** — yī yán jiǔ dǐng
## Characters


一 (yī) — one  
言 (yán) — word  
九 (jiǔ) — nine  
鼎 (dǐng) — tripods


## Origin
The idiom originates from ancient China, where tripods were considered symbols of power and authority. A ruler's word was seen as strong as nine tripods, signifying that a single promise could carry immense weight. This concept highlights the importance of integrity and trustworthiness in communication.

## Examples
- 他的承诺一言九鼎。<br>His promise is worth nine tripods.
- 在商业中，一言九鼎是成功的关键。<br>In business, a promise is the key to success.
- 朋友之间，一言九鼎更是珍贵。<br>Between friends, a promise is even more precious.

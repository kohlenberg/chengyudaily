Welcome to Chengyu Bites — your quick summary on Chinese 成语. Today, we explore the pursuit of perfection in Chinese culture.

## Overview
Perfect in every way; flawless.

## Phrase
**尽善尽美** — jìn shàn jìn měi
## Characters


尽 (jìn) — to the utmost  
善 (shàn) — good  
尽 (jìn) — to the utmost  
美 (měi) — beautiful


## Origin
The idiom 尽善尽美 originates from ancient Chinese philosophy, emphasizing the importance of striving for excellence in every aspect of life. It reflects a belief that one should not only seek to be good but also beautiful in actions and intentions. This pursuit of perfection has influenced various fields, from art to governance, throughout Chinese history.

## Examples
- 例如，艺术家追求尽善尽美的作品。<br>An artist strives for a flawless piece of work.
- 在婚礼上，新娘希望她的一切尽善尽美。<br>At the wedding, the bride hopes everything will be perfect.
- 他在工作中追求尽善尽美。<br>He pursues perfection in his work.

## Overview
To hit a stone with an egg; a futile effort.

## Phrase
**以卵击石** — yǐ luǎn jī shí
## Characters


以 (yǐ) — to use  
卵 (luǎn) — egg  
击 (jī) — to hit  
石 (shí) — stone


## Origin
This idiom originates from ancient Chinese literature, illustrating the futility of challenging something far stronger than oneself. The image of an egg attempting to strike a stone is both vivid and poignant, reminding us of the importance of recognizing our limits. Those who ignore this wisdom often face dire consequences.

## Examples
- 他以卵击石，根本不是对手。<br>He is hitting a stone with an egg; he is simply no match for the opponent.
- 面对强敌，他们的努力就像以卵击石。<br>Against a strong enemy, their efforts are like hitting a stone with an egg.
- 别以为你能以卵击石，还是想想其他办法吧。<br>Don’t think you can hit a stone with an egg; better consider other ways.

## Closing
以卵击石 means to attempt the impossible or to take on a much stronger foe.

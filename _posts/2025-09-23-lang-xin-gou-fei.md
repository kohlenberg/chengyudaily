Welcome to Chengyu Bites — your quick summary on Chinese 成语. Today, we delve into a phrase that captures the essence of betrayal and ingratitude.

## Overview
Wolf's heart and dog's lungs; describes someone who is cruel and ungrateful.

## Phrase
**狼心狗肺** — láng xīn gǒu fèi
## Characters


狼 (láng) — wolf  
心 (xīn) — heart  
狗 (gǒu) — dog  
肺 (fèi) — lungs


## Origin
This idiom originates from a fable about a wolf and a dog. The wolf, known for its cunning and ferocity, was contrasted with a loyal dog. The story emphasizes that while the dog is faithful, the wolf represents betrayal. Hence, someone with a 'wolf's heart and dog's lungs' is someone who shows ungratefulness despite receiving kindness.

## Examples
- 他狼心狗肺，背叛了朋友。<br>He has a wolf's heart and dog’s lungs, betraying his friends.
- 她对我如此无情，真是狼心狗肺。<br>She is so heartless to me; she truly has a wolf's heart and dog’s lungs.
- 这种狼心狗肺的人不值得信任。<br>People like this, with a wolf's heart and dog's lungs, are not trustworthy.

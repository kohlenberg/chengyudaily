Welcome to Chengyu Bites — your quick summary on Chinese 成语. Ever felt torn between two great choices? This idiom captures that dilemma perfectly. The phrase is: 鱼与熊掌不可兼得, yú yǔ xióng zhǎng bù kě jiān dé.

## Overview
You cannot have both fish and bear's paw.

## Phrase
**鱼与熊掌不可兼得** — yú yǔ xióng zhǎng bù kě jiān dé
## Characters


鱼 (yú) — fish  
与 (yǔ) — and  
熊 (xióng) — bear  
掌 (zhǎng) — paw  
不 (bù) — not  
可 (kě) — can  
兼得 (jiān dé) — have both


## Origin
This idiom originates from a fable about a person who had to choose between enjoying a delicious fish or a bear's paw, which was considered a delicacy. It illustrates the idea that sometimes, in life, we must make tough decisions and cannot have everything we want. The phrase serves as a reminder of the importance of prioritizing what truly matters to us.

## Examples
- 你想去旅行，但也想存钱，鱼与熊掌不可兼得。<br>You want to travel, but you also want to save money; you can't have both.
- 在工作和休息之间，你必须选择，鱼与熊掌不可兼得。<br>Between work and rest, you must choose; you can't have both.
- 如果你想追求梦想，就得放弃一些舒适，鱼与熊掌不可兼得。<br>If you want to pursue your dreams, you have to give up some comfort; you can't have both.

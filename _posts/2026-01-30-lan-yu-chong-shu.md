Ever wondered how a simple musical term became a warning against deception?

## Overview
Filling the ranks with inferior quality.

## Phrase
**滥竽充数** — làn yú chōng shù
## Characters


滥 (làn) — to mix or to flood  
竽 (yú) — a type of bamboo reed instrument  
充 (chōng) — to fill  
数 (shù) — number or to count


## Origin
In ancient China, there was a king who loved music. He hired a group of musicians to play for him, including a man who could not actually play the instrument he pretended to know, the 笙 (shēng). He simply blended in with the others, making it hard to identify his lack of skill. Eventually, the king discovered the truth and realized that not everyone in his court was genuinely talented.

## Examples
- 他滥竽充数，根本不会做这个工作。<br>He fills the ranks with inferior quality; he can't do this job at all.
- 别让那些滥竽充数的人进公司。<br>Don't let those unqualified people into the company.
- 我们需要真正的专家，而不是滥竽充数的人。<br>We need real experts, not impostors.

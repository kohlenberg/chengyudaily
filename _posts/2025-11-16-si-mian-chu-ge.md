This idiom describes a situation where one feels trapped and outnumbered.

## Overview
Surrounded by enemies, feeling isolated.

## Phrase
**四面楚歌** — sì miàn chǔ gē
## Characters


四 (sì) — four  
面 (miàn) — sides  
楚 (chǔ) — Chu (a historical state)  
歌 (gē) — song


## Origin
This idiom originates from a historical event during the Qin dynasty when the army of Xiang Yu was encircled by the enemy. As they were outnumbered and in despair, they heard songs from the Chu region sung by the enemy soldiers, which reminded them of their homeland and their hopeless situation. This led to the phrase becoming synonymous with feeling trapped and desolate.

## Examples
- 他现在的处境就像四面楚歌。<br>His current situation is like being surrounded on all sides by enemies.
- 在会议上，她感到四面楚歌，无法表达自己的意见。<br>At the meeting, she felt surrounded and unable to express her opinion.
- 面对竞争对手，他觉得四面楚歌，压力很大。<br>Facing his competitors, he feels trapped and under a lot of pressure.

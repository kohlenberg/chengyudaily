## Overview
Not limited to one style; open to diverse approaches.

## Phrase
**不拘一格** — bù jū yī gé
## Characters


不 (bù) — not  
拘 (jū) — to restrict  
一 (yī) — one  
格 (gé) — style or standard


## Origin
This idiom originates from the world of art and literature, where it was often said that true creativity cannot be confined to a single format or style. Great thinkers and artists throughout history have embraced diversity in their work, encouraging others to do the same. Thus, this phrase encourages innovation and the acceptance of various perspectives.

## Examples
- 他在设计上不拘一格，创造了许多独特的作品。<br>He is not limited to one style in his designs, creating many unique works.
- 教师应该不拘一格，以适应不同学生的需求。<br>Teachers should be flexible in their methods to meet the needs of different students.
- 这家公司鼓励员工不拘一格地思考，推动创新。<br>This company encourages employees to think outside the box, fostering innovation.

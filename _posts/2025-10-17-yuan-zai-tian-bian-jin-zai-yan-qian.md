> Ever felt that something you want is just out of reach?

## Overview
Distant in the sky, close at hand; often refers to things that seem far away but are actually near.

## Phrase
**远在天边，近在眼前** — yuǎn zài tiān biān, jìn zài yǎn qián
## Characters


远 (yuǎn) — distant  
在 (zài) — at  
天 (tiān) — sky  
边 (biān) — edge  
， ((,)) — (comma)  
近 (jìn) — near  
在 (zài) — at  
眼 (yǎn) — eye  
前 (qián) — front


## Origin
This idiom originates from ancient Chinese literature, where it was often used to describe aspirations or dreams that feel unattainable. People would express how their goals seemed as distant as the horizon yet were much closer than they realized. It highlights the irony of human perception regarding our desires and achievements.

## Examples
- 这份工作可能远在天边，但其实你离它很近。<br>This job may seem far away, but actually, you're very close to it.
- 他想要的成功，远在天边，近在眼前。<br>The success he desires seems distant, yet it’s right in front of him.
- 你的梦想远在天边，但努力后就会发现它很近。<br>Your dream seems far away, but after working hard, you'll find it's quite near.

## Closing
Remember, 远在天边，近在眼前 means that what seems far might actually be close.

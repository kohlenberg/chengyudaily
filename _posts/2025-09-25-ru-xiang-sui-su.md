## Overview
When in Rome, do as the Romans do.

## Phrase
**入乡随俗** — rù xiāng suí sú
## Characters


入 (rù) — to enter  
乡 (xiāng) — hometown or countryside  
随 (suí) — to follow  
俗 (sú) — customs or traditions


## Origin
This idiom originates from the idea that when travelers visit a new place, they should respect and adapt to the local culture and traditions. It emphasizes the importance of cultural sensitivity and understanding. Over time, this saying has become a reminder for people to be open-minded and flexible when encountering different customs.

## Examples
- 到了外国，记得入乡随俗。<br>When you arrive in a foreign country, remember to follow the local customs.
- 在南方，吃饭要用筷子，入乡随俗。<br>In the south, you should use chopsticks for meals, when in Rome, do as the Romans do.
- 学习当地的语言也是入乡随俗。<br>Learning the local language is also part of adapting to local customs.

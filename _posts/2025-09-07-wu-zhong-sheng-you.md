## Overview
to create something out of nothing; to fabricate

## Phrase
**无中生有** — wú zhōng shēng yǒu
## Characters


无 (wú) — no; none  
中 (zhōng) — middle; center  
生 (shēng) — to give birth; to produce  
有 (yǒu) — to have; there is


## Origin
This idiom originates from ancient Chinese literature, where a master was able to conjure up stories and ideas that seemed to appear from thin air. It emphasizes creativity and the power of imagination. Over time, it was used to describe situations where people invent stories or situations that are not based on reality.

## Examples
- 他总是无中生有，编造各种谎言。<br>He always creates stories out of nothing, making up all sorts of lies.
- 这幅画让人感觉无中生有，充满了幻想。<br>This painting feels like it creates something out of nothing, full of imagination.
- 不要无中生有，事实才是最重要的。<br>Don't fabricate stories; the truth is what matters most.

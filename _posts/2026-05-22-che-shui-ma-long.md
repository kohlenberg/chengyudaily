## Overview
Heavy traffic; a bustling scene.

## Phrase
**车水马龙** — chē shuǐ mǎ lóng
## Characters


车 (chē) — car  
水 (shuǐ) — water  
马 (mǎ) — horse  
龙 (lóng) — dragon


## Origin
This idiom originates from the busy streets of ancient China, where the sight of countless carriages and horses created a lively and chaotic atmosphere. It was said that the roads were so crowded that it resembled water flowing continuously. This vivid imagery has stuck around, symbolizing not just traffic, but also vibrant activity in any setting.

## Examples
- 大街上车水马龙。<br>The main street is bustling with heavy traffic.
- 在节日的时候，市场总是车水马龙。<br>The market is always bustling during the festivals.
- 这座城市的夜生活真是车水马龙。<br>The nightlife in this city is truly vibrant and lively.

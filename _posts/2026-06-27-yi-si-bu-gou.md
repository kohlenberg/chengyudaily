## Overview
Meticulously attentive to detail; leaving no stone unturned.

## Phrase
**一丝不苟** — yī sī bù gǒu
## Characters


一 (yī) — one  
丝 (sī) — thread  
不 (bù) — not  
苟 (gǒu) — careless or sloppy


## Origin
This idiom comes from the meticulous standards of ancient Chinese craftsmen, who would ensure that even the smallest thread in their work was perfectly placed. A single loose thread could ruin the entire piece, symbolizing the importance of attention to detail in all endeavors. Over time, this notion evolved into a phrase that encourages thoroughness in any task.

## Examples
- 他做事一丝不苟。<br>He does his work with meticulous attention to detail.
- 这篇文章写得一丝不苟。<br>This article is written with exceptional precision.
- 老师对学生的作业要求一丝不苟。<br>The teacher expects meticulous attention to detail in the students' assignments.

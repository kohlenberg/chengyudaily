Today, we explore the importance of precision in our actions.[break 1s]The phrase is: 一丝不苟, yī sī bù gǒu.[break 0.5s]一 (yī) - one;[break 0.5s]丝 (sī) - thread;[break 0.5s]不 (bù) - not;[break 0.5s]苟 (gǒu) - careless or sloppy.[break 1s]The idiom again: 一丝不苟 means literally 'not a thread careless', and figuratively it describes someone who is very meticulous and attentive to details.[break 1s]Here’s the story behind it:[break 1.5s]This idiom comes from the meticulous standards of ancient Chinese craftsmen, who would ensure that even the smallest thread in their work was perfectly placed. A single loose thread could ruin the entire piece, symbolizing the importance of attention to detail in all endeavors. Over time, this notion evolved into a phrase that encourages thoroughness in any task. [break 1.5s]Example 1: 他做事一丝不苟。[break 1s]He does his work with meticulous attention to detail.[break 1s]Example 2: 这篇文章写得一丝不苟。[break 1s]This article is written with exceptional precision.[break 1s]Example 3: 老师对学生的作业要求一丝不苟。[break 1s]The teacher expects meticulous attention to detail in the students' assignments.[break 1s]In summary, 一丝不苟 means to be meticulously attentive to detail.

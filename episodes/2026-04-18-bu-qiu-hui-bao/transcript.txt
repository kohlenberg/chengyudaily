Welcome to Chengyu Bites — your quick summary on Chinese 成语. Let's explore the beauty of selfless giving in Chinese culture. [break 1s] The phrase is: 不求回报, bù qiú huí bào. [break 1s] 不 (bù) - not [break 0.5s] 求 (qiú) - to seek [break 0.5s] 回 (huí) - to return [break 0.5s] 报 (bào) - to repay [break 1s] So, 不求回报 literally means 'not seeking return' and figuratively means 'to give without expecting anything in return.' [break 1s] Here’s the story behind it: [break 1.5s] This idiom originated from the idea that true kindness does not anticipate rewards. Ancient philosophers believed that genuine acts of kindness should come from the heart, not for personal gain. Over time, this concept became a valued principle in Chinese culture, emphasizing selflessness in relationships and actions. [break 1.5s] Example one: 他帮助别人，不求回报。 [break 1s] He helps others without expecting anything in return. [break 1s] Example two: 她总是为社区服务，不求回报。 [break 1s] She always serves the community without seeking any reward. [break 1s] Example three: 朋友之间的帮助应该是不求回报的。 [break 1s] Help among friends should be without expectations of return. [break 1s] So, 不求回报 means to give without expecting anything in return.

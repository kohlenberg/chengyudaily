[break 1s] 无 (wú) - no; none [break 0.5s] 中 (zhōng) - middle; center [break 0.5s] 生 (shēng) - to give birth; to produce [break 0.5s] 有 (yǒu) - to have; there is [break 1s] The full idiom again: 无中生有 - literally 'from nothing, produce something', figuratively meaning to create something out of nothing or to fabricate. [break 1s] Here’s the story behind it: [break 1.5s] This idiom originates from ancient Chinese literature, where a master was able to conjure up stories and ideas that seemed to appear from thin air. It emphasizes creativity and the power of imagination. Over time, it was used to describe situations where people invent stories or situations that are not based on reality. [break 1.5s] Example 1: 他总是无中生有，编造各种谎言。 [break 1s] He always creates stories out of nothing, making up all sorts of lies. [break 1s] Example 2: 这幅画让人感觉无中生有，充满了幻想。 [break 1s] This painting feels like it creates something out of nothing, full of imagination. [break 1s] Example 3: 不要无中生有，事实才是最重要的。 [break 1s] Don't fabricate stories; the truth is what matters most. [break 1s] So, that's 无中生有, meaning to create something out of nothing.

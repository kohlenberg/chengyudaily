Let's find out![break 1s]The phrase is: 如虎添翼, rú hǔ tiān yì.[break 0.5s]如 (rú) - like, as[break 0.5s]虎 (hǔ) - tiger[break 0.5s]添 (tiān) - to add[break 0.5s]翼 (yì) - wings[break 0.5s]The full idiom is 如虎添翼, which literally means 'like a tiger adding wings' and figuratively means to give someone or something a powerful boost.[break 1s]Here’s the story behind it:[break 1.5s]This idiom originates from ancient Chinese literature, where tigers were seen as powerful and fierce creatures. The concept of adding wings to such a creature symbolizes enhancing its strength and ability. It suggests that when a strong force receives additional support, it becomes even more unstoppable. This imagery has resonated through the ages, reminding us of the power of synergy.[break 1.5s]Example 1: 他在比赛中如虎添翼，轻松赢得了冠军。[break 1s]He was like a tiger with wings in the competition, easily winning the championship.[break 1s]Example 2: 新的科技让公司的发展如虎添翼。[break 1s]The new technology has given the company's growth a powerful boost.[break 1s]Example 3: 有了这些支持，他们的计划如虎添翼。[break 1s]With this support, their plans are like a tiger with wings.[break 1s]So, that's 如虎添翼, adding wings to a tiger, meaning a powerful boost.

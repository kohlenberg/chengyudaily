Welcome to Chengyu Bites — your quick summary on Chinese 成语. Ever heard the phrase that encourages you to take action at the right time? [break 1s] The phrase is: 趁热打铁, chèn rè dǎ tiě. [break 0.5s] 趁 (chèn) - take advantage of [break 0.5s] 热 (rè) - hot [break 0.5s] 打 (dǎ) - strike [break 0.5s] 铁 (tiě) - iron [break 1s] So, the full idiom is 趁热打铁, which literally means 'strike while the iron is hot.' Figuratively, it encourages us to seize opportunities when they arise. [break 1s] Here’s the story behind it: [break 1.5s] This idiom comes from the ancient practice of blacksmithing. When iron is heated, it becomes malleable and easier to shape. If a blacksmith waits too long to strike, the iron cools and hardens, making it difficult to work with. Thus, it teaches us the importance of acting promptly and taking advantage of favorable conditions. [break 1.5s] Example one: 赶快做决定，趁热打铁！ [break 1s] Make the decision quickly, strike while the iron is hot! [break 1s] Example two: 我们要趁热打铁，抓住这个机会。 [break 1s] We need to seize the moment and grab this opportunity. [break 1s] Example three: 他建议我们趁热打铁，尽快完成项目。 [break 1s] He suggested that we act swiftly and finish the project quickly. [break 1s] So, that's 趁热打铁, which means to strike while the iron is hot.

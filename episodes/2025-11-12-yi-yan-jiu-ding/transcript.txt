Welcome to Chengyu Bites — your quick summary on Chinese 成语. Today, we explore the weight of a single promise. [break 1s] The phrase is: 一言九鼎, yī yán jiǔ dǐng. [break 1s] 一 (yī) - one [break 0.5s] 言 (yán) - word [break 0.5s] 九 (jiǔ) - nine [break 0.5s] 鼎 (dǐng) - tripods [break 1s] So, when we put it all together, 一言九鼎 literally means 'one word nine tripods.' Figuratively, it signifies that a promise holds great weight and importance. [break 1s] Here’s the story behind it: [break 1.5s] The idiom originates from ancient China, where tripods were considered symbols of power and authority. A ruler's word was seen as strong as nine tripods, signifying that a single promise could carry immense weight. This concept highlights the importance of integrity and trustworthiness in communication. [break 1.5s] Example 1: 他的承诺一言九鼎。 [break 1s] His promise is worth nine tripods. [break 1s] Example 2: 在商业中，一言九鼎是成功的关键。 [break 1s] In business, a promise is the key to success. [break 1s] Example 3: 朋友之间，一言九鼎更是珍贵。 [break 1s] Between friends, a promise is even more precious. [break 1s] So, once again, 一言九鼎 means that a promise is invaluable. Thank you for listening to Chengyu Bites! See you next time for another idiom. [break 1s]

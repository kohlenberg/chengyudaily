Today, we’re diving into a term that describes perfection in a unique way.[break 1s] The phrase is: 无懈可击, wú xiè kě jī.[break 0.5s] 无 (wú) means 'without'[break 0.5s] 懈 (xiè) means 'laziness' or 'weakness'[break 0.5s] 可 (kě) means 'can' or 'able to'[break 0.5s] 击 (jī) means 'hit' or 'strike'[break 1s] So, the full idiom 无懈可击 literally means 'cannot hit any flaws' and figuratively refers to something being flawless or perfect.[break 1s] Here’s the story behind it:[break 1.5s] This idiom originates from a story about a skilled archer. The archer was so precise that no one could find any mistakes in his technique. No matter how hard others tried to criticize or point out errors, they simply couldn't. Thus, the phrase evolved to represent anything that is impeccable. [break 1.5s] Example one: 他的计划无懈可击。[break 1s] His plan is flawless. [break 1s] Example two: 她的演讲无懈可击。[break 1s] Her speech was without any flaws. [break 1s] Example three: 这幅画无懈可击。[break 1s] This painting is perfect. [break 1s] So, remember, 无懈可击 means flawless or without any loopholes. Thank you for listening to Chengyu Bites! See you next time for another idiom.[break 1s]

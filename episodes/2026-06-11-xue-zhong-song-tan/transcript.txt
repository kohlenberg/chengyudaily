Welcome to Chengyu Bites — your quick summary on Chinese 成语. Ever wondered how a simple gesture can warm someone's heart in tough times? [break 1s] The phrase is: 雪中送炭, xuě zhōng sòng tàn. [break 1s] 雪 (xuě) - snow [break 0.5s] 中 (zhōng) - in [break 0.5s] 送 (sòng) - to send [break 0.5s] 炭 (tàn) - coal [break 1s] So, the idiom again: 雪中送炭 means 'Delivering coal in the snow,' which figuratively means providing timely help to someone in need. [break 1s] Here’s the story behind it: [break 1.5s] In ancient China, during harsh winters, people would often struggle to find warmth. If someone brought coal to a family caught in a snowstorm, it was a lifesaver. This act of kindness inspired the idiom, highlighting the importance of helping others when they are in dire situations. [break 1.5s] Example one: 他在我最困难的时候给了我很多帮助，真是雪中送炭。 He helped me a lot when I was in the toughest times; he truly delivered coal in the snow. [break 1s] Example two: 朋友在我失业时支持我，实在是雪中送炭。 My friend supported me when I lost my job; it was indeed delivering coal in the snow. [break 1s] Example three: 医生在疫情期间无私奉献，真是雪中送炭。 The doctors selflessly dedicated themselves during the pandemic; they were truly delivering coal in the snow. [break 1s] So, remember: 雪中送炭 means providing timely help. Thank you for listening to Chengyu Bites! See you next time for another idiom. [break 1s]

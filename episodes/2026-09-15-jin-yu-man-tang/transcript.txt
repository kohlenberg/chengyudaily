Welcome to Chengyu Bites — your quick summary on Chinese 成语. Ever wondered how a phrase about wealth can also reflect prosperity in life? [break 1s] The phrase is: 金玉满堂, jīn yù mǎn táng. [break 1s] 
金 (jīn) - gold [break 0.5s] 
玉 (yù) - jade [break 0.5s] 
满 (mǎn) - full [break 0.5s] 
堂 (táng) - hall [break 0.5s] 
Full idiom: 金玉满堂 literally means 'a hall full of gold and jade' and figuratively refers to being rich and prosperous. [break 1s] 
Here’s the story behind it: [break 1.5s] In ancient times, gold and jade were symbols of wealth and status. The phrase originated from a historical context where the rich would display these precious items in their homes to showcase their affluence. Over time, it evolved into a metaphor for abundance in both material wealth and life’s blessings. [break 1.5s] 
Example 1: 他的家里金玉满堂，生活非常富裕。 
His home is filled with gold and jade; they live very richly. [break 1s] 
Example 2: 她的成功让我们都感到金玉满堂。 
Her success makes us all feel prosperous. [break 1s] 
Example 3: 这座城市金玉满堂，吸引了很多投资者。 
This city is rich and prosperous, attracting many investors. [break 1s] 
So, remember, 金玉满堂 means a hall filled with gold and jade, symbolizing wealth and abundance. Thank you for listening to Chengyu Bites! See you next time for another idiom. [break 1s]

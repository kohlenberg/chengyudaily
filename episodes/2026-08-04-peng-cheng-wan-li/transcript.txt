Today, we explore how a mythical bird symbolizes limitless opportunities.[break 1s] The phrase is: 鹏程万里, péng chéng wàn lǐ.[break 0.5s] 鹏 (péng) - a mythical bird, symbolizes great ambition.[break 0.5s] 程 (chéng) - journey or distance.[break 0.5s] 万 (wàn) - ten thousand, representing a large number.[break 0.5s] 里 (lǐ) - a unit of distance, akin to a mile.[break 1s] In full, 鹏程万里 literally means 'the bird's journey is ten thousand miles,' figuratively expressing a great journey ahead or vast potential.[break 1s] Here’s the story behind it:[break 1.5s] The idiom originates from the ancient text Zhuangzi, where a giant bird called the Peng flies high in the sky, traversing great distances. This imagery evokes the idea of pursuing one's dreams and ambitions without limits. The Peng represents not just any journey but an extraordinary one, inspiring those who hear it to aspire for greatness.[break 1.5s] Example 1: 他在事业上鹏程万里。  He has a bright future in his career.[break 1s] Example 2: 这位年轻的作家鹏程万里。 This young writer has immense potential.[break 1s] Example 3: 她的计划真是鹏程万里。 Her plans are truly ambitious.[break 1s] So, we’ve explored 鹏程万里, meaning a great journey ahead; vast potential.

Today, we explore a phrase that captures the essence of global connections.[break 1s]The phrase is: 五湖四海, wǔ hú sì hǎi.[break 0.5s]五 (wǔ) means 'five'[break 0.5s]湖 (hú) means 'lakes'[break 0.5s]四 (sì) means 'four'[break 0.5s]海 (hǎi) means 'seas'[break 0.5s]Full idiom: 五湖四海 literally means 'five lakes and four seas', and figuratively it refers to all corners of the world; everywhere.[break 1s]Here’s the story behind it:[break 1.5s]This idiom originates from the classic text 'Shui Hu Zhuan', which describes a gathering of people from various regions. The idea is that no matter where you come from, everyone can connect and become friends, just like how lakes and seas are interconnected. This reflects a spirit of unity and inclusivity.[break 1.5s]Example 1: 他在五湖四海都有朋友。[break 1s]He has friends all over the world.[break 1s]Example 2: 这位旅行者走遍了五湖四海。[break 1s]This traveler has been everywhere.[break 1s]Example 3: 我们的文化影响五湖四海。[break 1s]Our culture influences all corners of the globe.[break 1s]So, remember, 五湖四海 means 'all corners of the world'; it's about connection and friendship.

It evokes a calm and pleasant atmosphere, often used in poetry to set a serene mood. Over time, it has become a common expression in everyday conversation to describe delightful weather. [break 1.5s] Example 1: 今天天气真好，风和日丽！ [break 1s] The weather is great today, with a gentle breeze and beautiful sun!

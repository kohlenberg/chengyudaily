Today, we’re diving into a phrase that describes someone who goes along with the flow.[break 1s] The phrase is: 酱油瓶子, jiàng yóu píng zi.[break 0.5s] 酱 (jiàng) - sauce[break 0.5s] 油 (yóu) - oil[break 0.5s] 瓶 (píng) - bottle[break 0.5s] 子 (zi) - a suffix for objects[break 1s] So, 酱油瓶子 literally means 'soy sauce bottle' and figuratively refers to someone who is easily manipulated or lacks their own opinions.[break 1s] Here’s the story behind it:[break 1.5s] In traditional Chinese homes, soy sauce was stored in bottles. These bottles would often be swayed back and forth, much like a person who simply follows the opinions of others without a firm stance. Over time, this image evolved into a metaphor for those who are indecisive or easily influenced. It’s a playful way to describe someone who doesn't stand firm on their own beliefs.[break 1.5s] Example 1: 你总是跟着别人的意见，真是个酱油瓶子。 [break 1s] You always follow others’ opinions; you really are a soy sauce bottle. [break 1s] Example 2: 她在团队中就像酱油瓶子，缺乏主见。 [break 1s] She is like a soy sauce bottle in the team, lacking her own views. [break 1s] Example 3: 不要当酱油瓶子，勇敢表达自己的想法。 [break 1s] Don’t be a soy sauce bottle; bravely express your own thoughts. [break 1s] So, that’s 酱油瓶子, meaning someone who is easily manipulated.

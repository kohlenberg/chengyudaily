Welcome to Chengyu Bites — your quick summary on Chinese 成语. Today, we explore a saying that teaches us to think carefully before acting. [break 1s] The phrase is: 投鼠忌器, tóu shǔ jì qì. [break 1s] 投 (tóu) - to throw [break 0.5s] 鼠 (shǔ) - mouse [break 0.5s] 忌 (jì) - to avoid or be cautious of [break 0.5s] 器 (qì) - tool or instrument [break 1s] So, 投鼠忌器 literally means 'to throw a mouse but be cautious of the instrument.' Figuratively, it means to avoid harming the innocent while trying to catch the guilty. [break 1s] Here’s the story behind it: [break 1.5s] This idiom comes from a story about a cat trying to catch a mouse. The cat was worried that if it threw something to hit the mouse, it might break the pottery nearby. So, it held back, realizing that its actions could harm more than just the mouse. This reflects the idea of being mindful of the consequences of our actions. [break 1.5s] Example one: 为了抓到小偷，他投鼠忌器，不想伤到无辜的人。 [break 1s] To catch the thief, he was cautious not to harm innocent people. [break 1s] Example two: 在处理这件事时，我们应该投鼠忌器，考虑后果。 [break 1s] When handling this matter, we should be careful to consider the consequences. [break 1s] Example three: 她在做决定时总是投鼠忌器，避免给别人带来麻烦。 [break 1s] She always avoids harm to others when making decisions. [break 1s] So remember 投鼠忌器 means to avoid harming the innocent while trying to catch the guilty. Thanks for listening to Chengyu Bites! See you next time for another idiom. [break 1s]

Let's dive in![break 1s] The phrase is: 风雨同舟, fēng yǔ tóng zhōu.[break 0.5s] 风, fēng, means 'wind'.[break 0.5s] 雨, yǔ, means 'rain'.[break 0.5s] 同, tóng, means 'together'.[break 0.5s] 舟, zhōu, means 'boat'.[break 1s] So, 风雨同舟 literally means 'wind and rain, same boat', and figuratively it means 'to share hardships together'.[break 1s] Here’s the story behind it:[break 1.5s] This idiom originates from a time when people had to band together to face natural disasters. It emphasizes the importance of unity and support during tough times. Just like being in a boat during a storm, we need to rely on one another to get through challenges together.[break 1.5s] Example 1: 在困难时期，我们应该风雨同舟。  During tough times, we should stand together. [break 1s] Example 2: 在这个项目中，大家风雨同舟，才能取得成功。  In this project, everyone must work together to achieve success. [break 1s] Example 3: 朋友们，风雨同舟，共同面对挑战！  Friends, let's share the hardships and face the challenges together! [break 1s] So, that's 风雨同舟, meaning to share hardships together.

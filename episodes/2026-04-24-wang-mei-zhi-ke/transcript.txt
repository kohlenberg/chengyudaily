Welcome to Chengyu Bites — your quick summary on Chinese 成语. Ever found yourself craving something that’s just out of reach? [break 1s] The phrase is: 望梅止渴, wàng méi zhǐ kě. [break 0.5s] 望 (wàng) - to gaze; [break 0.5s] 梅 (méi) - plum; [break 0.5s] 止 (zhǐ) - to stop; [break 0.5s] 渴 (kě) - thirsty. [break 1s] 望梅止渴, literally means 'gazing at plums to quench thirst,' and figuratively, it refers to using something desirable to motivate oneself during tough times. [break 1s] Here’s the story behind it: [break 1.5s] In ancient times, a weary traveler was feeling extremely thirsty. To ease his discomfort, he imagined a grove of plums nearby. Just thinking about the juicy fruit made his thirst feel more bearable and inspired him to keep going. This idiom reminds us that sometimes, envisioning a goal can help us push through challenges. [break 1.5s] Example one: 他为了减肥，常常想象自己吃到美味的水果。 [break 1s] To lose weight, he often imagines himself eating delicious fruits. [break 1s] Example two: 学生们在考试期间，想象毕业后的美好生活。 [break 1s] During exams, students envision the wonderful life after graduation. [break 1s] Example three: 在工作压力大时，她会想着假期的旅行来放松自己。 [break 1s] When work pressure is high, she thinks about her vacation trip to relax herself.

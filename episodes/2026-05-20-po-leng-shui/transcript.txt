This idiom perfectly captures that moment.[break 1s]The phrase is: 泼冷水, pō lěng shuǐ.[break 0.5s]泼, pō, means 'to splash' or 'to pour'.[break 0.5s]冷, lěng, means 'cold'.[break 0.5s]水, shuǐ, means 'water'.[break 0.5s]The full idiom again is 泼冷水, literally 'to splash cold water', and figuratively means 'to pour cold water on someone’s enthusiasm'.[break 1s]Here’s the story behind it:[break 1.5s]Imagine a hot summer day when someone is excited about going for a swim. Suddenly, someone splashes cold water on them, ruining the mood. This idiom conveys how negativity can dampen someone’s excitement or plans, much like that unexpected splash. It reminds us to be mindful of how our words can affect others.[break 1.5s]Example 1: 你总是泼冷水，别让大家失去信心！[break 1s]You always pour cold water on things; don’t let everyone lose confidence! [break 1s]Example 2: 她的评论真是泼冷水，让我很沮丧。[break 1s]Her comment was really cold water; it made me very frustrated.[break 1s]Example 3: 在这个项目上，请不要泼冷水，我们需要积极的反馈。[break 1s]On this project, please don’t pour cold water; we need positive feedback.[break 1s]So, the idiom is 泼冷水, which means to dampen enthusiasm.

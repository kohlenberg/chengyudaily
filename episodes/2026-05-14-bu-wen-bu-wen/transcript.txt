Welcome to Chengyu Bites — your quick summary on Chinese 成语. Ever felt like ignoring a problem is the best solution? Let's dive into this idiom! [break 1s] The phrase is: 不闻不问, bù wén bù wèn. [break 1s] 不 (bù) - not [break 0.5s] 闻 (wén) - to hear [break 0.5s] 不 (bù) - not [break 0.5s] 问 (wèn) - to ask [break 1s] So, 不闻不问 means literally 'not hear, not ask' and figuratively refers to being indifferent or unconcerned about something. [break 1s] Here’s the story behind it: [break 1.5s] This idiom dates back to ancient times when people would often turn a blind eye to social issues, preferring to stay uninvolved. It highlights a common tendency to avoid responsibility or engagement with pressing matters. Over time, it became a way to describe someone who is apathetic or indifferent. [break 1.5s] Example 1: 他对同事的困难不闻不问。 [break 1s] He is indifferent to his colleague's difficulties. [break 1s] Example 2: 社会问题不应不闻不问。 [break 1s] Social issues should not be ignored. [break 1s] Example 3: 这种态度让人觉得他不关心任何事情。 [break 1s] This attitude makes people feel he doesn't care about anything. [break 1s] So, remember, 不闻不问 means to be indifferent.

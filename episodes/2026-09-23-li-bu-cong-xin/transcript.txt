Let's explore that feeling in today’s idiom.[break 1s]The phrase is: 力不从心, lì bù cóng xīn.[break 0.5s]力 (lì) means 'strength' or 'power',[break 0.5s]不 (bù) means 'not',[break 0.5s]从 (cóng) means 'to follow',[break 0.5s]心 (xīn) means 'heart' or 'mind'.[break 1s]So, the full idiom 力不从心 literally means 'strength does not follow the heart', and figuratively, it suggests that one’s capabilities do not meet their aspirations.[break 1s]Here’s the story behind it:[break 1.5s]This idiom originates from the idea that sometimes, even if we have the desire and passion to do something, our physical strength or circumstances may not allow us to achieve it. It reflects a universal feeling of frustration when we encounter limitations that hinder our goals. This phrase resonates deeply in a fast-paced world where ambitions often exceed our capabilities. [break 1.5s]Example 1: 我想跑得很快，但我的腿力不从心。[break 1s]I want to run fast, but my legs can't keep up with my heart's desire.[break 1s]Example 2: 她的工作太多了，力不从心，感觉压力很大。[break 1s]She has too much work, unable to keep up, feeling very stressed.[break 1s]Example 3: 尽管他努力学习，但考试的结果还是让他力不从心。[break 1s]Even though he studies hard, the exam results still make him feel powerless.[break 1s]So, 力不从心 means being unable to act according to one's desires.

Ever wondered how impatience can ruin a good thing?[break 1s]The phrase is: 拔苗助长, bá miáo zhù zhǎng.[break 0.5s]拔 (bá) - pull out[break 0.5s]苗 (miáo) - seedling[break 0.5s]助 (zhù) - help[break 0.5s]长 (zhǎng) - grow[break 1s]So, 拔苗助长 literally means "to pull seedlings to help them grow," figuratively it warns against ruining things through excessive intervention.[break 1s]Here’s the story behind it:[break 1.5s]In ancient China, a farmer was eager for a good harvest. He thought that if he pulled up the seedlings to help them grow faster, he would get a bigger yield. Unfortunately, his impatience led to the destruction of the plants, and he ended up with nothing. This story teaches us that rushing things can often lead to failure.[break 1.5s]Example 1: 他总是想拔苗助长，结果总是失败。[break 1s]He always wants to rush things, and as a result, he always fails.[break 1s]Example 2: 教孩子时，拔苗助长只会让他们变得更加焦虑。[break 1s]When teaching children, pushing them too hard will only make them more anxious.[break 1s]Example 3: 在工作中，过度干预可能会导致团队效率降低，拔苗助长。[break 1s]In work, excessive intervention can lower team efficiency; it's like pulling seedlings to help them grow.[break 1s]So, 拔苗助长 means to spoil things by excessive intervention.

[break 1s] 八 (bā) means 'eight' [break 0.5s] 面 (miàn) means 'sides' or 'faces' [break 0.5s] 玲 (líng) means 'tinkling' or 'clever' [break 0.5s] 决 (lóng) means 'delicate' or 'exquisite'. [break 1s] So, 八面玲珑 literally means 'eight sides exquisite', describing someone who is versatile and adept at handling various situations. [break 1s] Here’s the story behind it: [break 1.5s] This idiom originates from the description of a skilled artisan who could create a beautiful, multi-faceted piece of jade, reflecting light from all angles. Just like that jade, a person who is 八面玲珑 can navigate social situations with ease and charm, making them well-liked in various circles. [break 1.5s] It emphasizes the ability to adapt and connect with different people effortlessly.

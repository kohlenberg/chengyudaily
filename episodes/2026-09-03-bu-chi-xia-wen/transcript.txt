Ever wondered how asking questions can show strength instead of weakness?[break 1s]The phrase is: 不耻下问, bù chǐ xià wèn.[break 0.5s]不 (bù) - not[break 0.5s]耻 (chǐ) - ashamed[break 0.5s]下 (xià) - below[break 0.5s]问 (wèn) - to ask[break 1s]So, 不耻下问 literally means "not ashamed to ask below" and figuratively, it means to be humble enough to seek knowledge from others, regardless of their status.[break 1s]Here’s the story behind it:[break 1.5s]This idiom comes from the teachings of Confucius, who emphasized the importance of learning from anyone, regardless of their social standing. He often encouraged his students to ask questions, even to those considered less knowledgeable. The idea was that true wisdom comes from being open and willing to learn.[break 1.5s]Example 1: 他不耻下问，向老师请教问题。[break 1s]He is not ashamed to ask questions of his teacher.[break 1s]Example 2: 在工作中，年轻人应该不耻下问，向前辈学习。[break 1s]In the workplace, young people should not be ashamed to ask and learn from their seniors.[break 1s]Example 3: 她总是鼓励学生不耻下问，追求知识。[break 1s]She always encourages students to be humble and seek knowledge.[break 1s]So, 不耻下问 means to be humble in seeking knowledge.

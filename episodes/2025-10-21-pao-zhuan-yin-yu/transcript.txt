Ever wondered how a small gesture can lead to great rewards?[break 1s]The phrase is: 抛砖引玉, pāo zhuān yǐn yù.[break 0.5s]抛, pāo, means 'to throw';[break 0.5s]砖, zhuān, means 'brick';[break 0.5s]引, yǐn, means 'to attract';[break 0.5s]玉, yù, means 'jade'.[break 1s]So, 抛砖引玉 literally means 'throwing a brick to attract jade' and figuratively means offering something of lesser value to gain something more valuable.[break 1s]Here’s the story behind it:[break 1.5s]This idiom originates from a story where a scholar used a simple stone to entice talented individuals to share their knowledge. By presenting a lesser gift, he encouraged others to offer their greater insights in return. This clever tactic highlighted the value of humility and the wisdom that can come from collaboration.[break 1.5s]Example 1: 他在会议上抛砖引玉，希望大家提出更好的建议。[break 1s]He threw out a simple idea at the meeting, hoping everyone would suggest better ones.[break 1s]Example 2: 她用自己的经历抛砖引玉，鼓励其他人分享故事。[break 1s]She shared her own experience to attract others to share their stories.[break 1s]Example 3: 通过抛砖引玉，他成功吸引了更多投资者的关注。[break 1s]By throwing out a small proposal, he successfully attracted more investors' attention.[break 1s]So, that’s 抛砖引玉, which means using a small offering to gain something greater.
